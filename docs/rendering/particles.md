# Zone Particles & Weather Emitters

> The zone particle runtime: Section 0x05 generators, 0x1F particle meshes, 0x21 sprite sheets, 0x07 routines, and weather emitters. Status and open work: [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) and GitHub Issues.

## Particle-mesh water surfaces (0x1F)

- **Step: 0x1F Particle-Mesh Water Surfaces (persistent):** `ParticleMeshDecoder` decodes Section 0x1F meshes; `ZoneDataLoader` turns every zone-anchored, infinite-life (max life span 0) Section 0x05 generator linked to a 0x1F/0x2E mesh into a `ZoneGeometry.EffectLayers` world effect (replacing the name-based Phase 2b water instancing). `ZoneTerrainRenderer` draws them with the particle modulate-2x stages, generator color/alpha/clock curves, UV scroll, per-vertex particle lighting, fog, distance fade (0x2E near/far), depth mask and the client's opaque snap for blended particle meshes. Restores Bibiki Bay's open sea (`umi1`), shoreline caustic bands (`umat`/`uma3`), sunset glints (`yuhi`) and hillside fog patches.

## Finite-life emitters

- **Step: Finite-Life Particle Emitters (surf & wave crests):** `ZoneParticleEmitter` (Core) simulates zone-anchored generators on the 60 Hz effect clock: emission cadence/variance, particles-per-emission, continuous singletons, life span + variance, the 0x05 repeat handler, the 0x0A emission cull, and the initializers/updaters zone water uses (velocity, acceleration, rotation/scale velocity, progress curves for position/rotation/scale/color/UV with initial-value seeding and cycles, constant UV scroll, clock color/alpha, 0x2E distance fade, daylight tint). `ParticleGeneratorDecoder` now keeps every opcode, keyframe link cycles and expiration handlers; curves resolve from the generator's own directory first. Auto-running generators outside weather directories run continuously; non-auto-running ones run when an ambient Section 0x07 routine in their directory starts them (`EffectRoutineDecoder`, e.g. Bibiki umi2/s000 rolls kwa1..kwa3 in; which routines are ambient: [How zone routines start](#how-zone-routines-start-210)). The decoder walks each command list to its op 0x00 with no command cap: a walk of all 22,643 routines in the zone and effect DATs (2026-09-28) found 9 longer than 128 commands, up to 266 (Pso'Xja `d_ga/effe/bari/s000` and `bar2/s000`; Full Moon Fountain `hana/hast` has 206), and every list ends on op 0x00 within its section (#80). `ZoneTerrainRenderer` pre-warms emitters on zone load and draws each live particle through the shared generator submit path.

## Opcode coverage

- **Step: Remaining Particle Coverage:** A survey of all 299 zone DATs drove the opcode set. `ZoneParticleEmitter` now keeps one transform record per slot and covers velocity/relative velocity and their variance, spherical spawn scatter (0x06/0x07/0x1F), rotation/scale variance, incremental rotation (0x3B), oscillation (0x3D-0x40 / 0x29-0x2B), dampening, velocity rotation, progress velocity, clock scale/rotation/position, integrated UV/rotation, angular-distance rotation, color variance and color transforms (0x17-0x1A / 0x0B-0x0C), double-range fade (0x48), occlusion probes (0x53), day-of-week and moon-phase tints. The colour transform rates (initializer 0x19, applied by updater 0x0B: `rate >> 7`, times half the frames) are colour bytes, so they are divided by 255 for the 0-1 particle colours (#192, 2026-10-02): read as 0-1 they cleared or saturated a particle within two frames, e.g. the 70443 sparkles (rate -16 on 0x58, 120-frame life) and zone 12's `r-lt` lights (720 / -128 / -432); 2,957 of the 133,149 zone generators use it. **Differs from xi-model-viewer:** its ColorTransformApplier adds the same byte-scale rate to 0-1 colours. The daylight tint uses the 0x2F model sun/moon lights. Zone sprite-sheet particles (Section 0x21, ~17k generators: lamp halos, sparkles, fountain spray) run as emitters with XYZ / XZ billboarding and per-particle card selection (0x0D / 0x45). Child generators run as child-only emitter layers: 0x3C once at birth, 0x44/0x53/0x6A streams for the parent's life (0x25 / 0x33 / 0x46), and expiration 0x01; 0x45 copies the parent position.
- **Sound generators (#39):** the 5,868 zone generators whose linked data is a sound pointer (ParticleLinkedDataType.Audio) are not drawn; ZoneSoundEmitterDecoder reads their sound, the init ops 0x4C (range: 32 far, f32 near), 0x6B (path: 0x4A section name) and 0x68 (time-of-day volume key: 0x19 curve name, applied by update op 0x43) and the audio engine plays them ([audio.md](../design/audio.md#zone-effect-audio-39)).

## Weather emitters and routines

- **Step: Weather Emitters & Point Lights:** Looping ambient routines at their authored total length matched retail side by side on Bibiki Bay (2026-09-24).
  - *Chunk 1: Weather emitters.* Auto-running generators in weather directories (rain, snow, splashes, fireflies, `thdr/lig1`/`lig2` lightning bolts and their children) run as zone emitters gated on the active weather (a weather change clears them). Weather generators emit a third of their authored count (doubled first when batched); batched generators (`0x6B` flag `0x20`) emit one particle whose sub-particles take the spawn scatter, relative velocity, drift and dampening and draw once each at their world offset. Camera-following generators (setup flag `0x04`) track the camera plus their base; camera-anchored ones (render-state `0x0400`) stay where the camera was at birth. Birth daylight tint `0x90` (strongest model light) keeps rain mist (`smok`) dim under overcast, matching a Windower capture in Carpenters' Landing. Camera-oriented `0x1F` spawn shells face the view; Movement / MovementHorizontal / Camera billboards orient along the particle's last movement or toward the eye (movement ignored when batched). Sky-layer generators (clouds, sun, celestial shells and sprites) are excluded, and per-draw frustum culling keeps Xarcabard's snow at ~2.6k draws. Verified offscreen: Carpenters' Landing rain and thunder, Uleguerand snow; Bibiki unchanged.
  - Verified against retail (2026-09-24): rain density matches Windower; billboard-None rain cards are drawn flat in world space as the reference does.
  - *Caves:* camera-following and camera-anchored weather stops emitting while the floor under the camera belongs to a placement linked to a sub-environment (ZoneDef record `+0x4C`, e.g. `ev01`); verified offscreen in an Uleguerand Range cave (snow stops) and outdoors (snow falls).
  - *Child orientation:* transform-following child streams (`0x33`, and `0x46` billboarded toward the camera) place children in the parent particle's rotated, scaled frame (X-Y-Z rotation order, parent movement/camera billboards) and track the live parent while they follow their generator; parent-copy initializers `0x46` velocity, `0x47`/`0x79` rotation, `0x48` color, `0x49` scale, `0x4A` UV and `0x9B` position.
  - *Lightning strikes:* weather routines shorter than 1000 frames (`kmi*`, `kam*`, `kami`: 0-480 frames) without an op 0x52 timer form per-directory groups (`WeatherRoutinePlayer`) that play one random routine at a time under their weather; longer weather routines (birds, butterflies, fireflies, ducks: 1890-9000 frames) loop like zone ambient routines. The pause between strikes (4-15 s) is an estimate; an exact retail match was judged unnecessary. Since #81 the timed ones (most `kmi1` / `kmi2` strikes, the ducks and birds) play on their own op 0x52 clocks instead ([How zone routines start](#how-zone-routines-start-210)).

## How zone routines start (#210)

- **Step: On-demand zone routines ([#210](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/210), 2026-10-03, tested in-game).** Until now `ZoneDataLoader` looped every routine of a zone DAT that starts a non-auto-running generator. In Alzadaal Undersea Ruins (zone 72) the Runic Portals' routines (`d_at/effe/pba1/1pba` and `1pbb`, `d_at/effe/2pba`) looped too and piled up: `1pa2` (300 frames) re-armed `g0a1` / `g0c2`, and `s104` (0 frames) re-armed its pillars `tw21`-`tw24` every frame, since the emitter treated a loop as at least 1 frame.
- **The rule.** The routine header has three command-list offsets (+0x10, +0x14, +0x18); the timeline is the second. A routine the client starts on zone load has op 0x01 as the whole third list (with 0x100 at +4), or op 0x52 in its timeline (a replay on the client's own timer inside a Vana'diel clock window: birds, butterflies, lightning; its operands and timing since #81 are below). A third-list routine loops at its own length, together with the routines it starts (op 0x03 once at its frame, op 0x73 repeating at the child's length to the end of the cycle; references resolve in the routine's directory, then its parents). Every other routine waits for a trigger. A routine of 0 frames never loops: scheduled, it runs once; started repeating (0x73), it runs once. Weather directories keep the rule they had: all their routines play under their weather (the short ones at random, [Weather emitters and routines](#weather-emitters-and-routines)). Actor and scene DATs are unchanged: actors play their routines by name.
- **Survey (2026-10-03, all 19,176 routines in zone DATs 0-299, a temporary test on the retail install).** The first list is always empty. The third list is empty in 18,759 routines and exactly `01` in 417 (all outside weather directories, Bibiki Bay's `umi2/s000`, `s001`, `umi3`, `umi5` and `nami/ckem` among them; none in a weather directory). Op 0x52 is in 479 routines, 232 of them in weather directories; no routine carries both, and no other routine starts a 0x52 routine. Of the 2,239 routines that started a non-auto-running emitter or point light under the old rule, 760 keep running as roots (412 with the third-list loop in 74 zones, 348 with op 0x52 in 51 zones, 113 of these in weather directories), 13 keep running because a root starts them (Manaclipper's `shio/alls` starts `sio1` / `sio2` at its frames instead of their looping every 250 / 335 frames; Aht Urhgan Whitegate's `funs/redf` fires, started by the 0x52 routines `on01` / `off1`; Mount Zhayolm's `krav/tim1` starts `wkg1`, which starts `wkg2`), 118 short weather routines stay random, and 1,348 in 95 zones stop running by themselves: 690 under door entities (`<zone>/door/_xxx/...`, e.g. Alzadaal's `door/_20t/door/stat`), the rest set pieces such as the Runic Portals (`1pba`, `1pbb`, `2pba`), Chamber of Oracles' event effects (`evnt`), warp and crystal effects (`it_i` / `it_o`, `cr_o`, `mt_d`), door effects (`dor1`-`dor3`) and boats (`hune`). One root has 0 frames (zone 192 `t_st/tst`). Candidate rules checked against the same data: "started by another routine" (only 13 children of roots; 473 others are children of on-demand routines, e.g. the portal's `s102` / `s103`), "kills with 0x1E / 0x5F" (the portal's `1pak`, but 0-frame idles like `1pa1` kill nothing) and "0 frames" (187 looped routines, all on demand by the rule above) each describe only part of the on-demand set; the routine names follow no pattern the client could use.
- **Evidence for the trigger.** The server names the routines: S2C 0x039 (`GP_SERV_COMMAND_MAPSCHEDULOR`, XiPackets `world/server/0x0039`: "additional environment/map animations") carries a routine FourCC, and LandSandBoat sends `1pa1`, `1pb1` and `2pb1` after every Alzadaal zone-in (`scripts/zones/Alzadaal_Undersea_Ruins/Zone.lua`, `entityVisualPacket` → `GP_SERV_COMMAND_MAPSCHEDULOR`): the portals' idle state, 0 frames each. Other zones' scripts send their own (`lop1`, `byc1`-`byc8`, `kil1`, `door`, ...). xim's autorun heuristic reads the same third-list op 0x01 and op 0x52 as auto-starting (xi-tools `docs/fx/effect_system.md` "Triggering & autoRun"); its third rule, a directory with exactly one routine, is not used: Alzadaal's `2pba` and Bibiki's `ligh` hold several routines and the single-routine directories include on-demand ones. **Beyond xi-tools:** the census above (xim calls its rule a heuristic).
- **Runtime.** Routines with effects outside weather and door directories go to `ZoneGeometry.MapRoutines` (`ZoneRoutineLibrary`, by directory path; the on-demand ones' non-auto-running generators get idle emitters). `EntityPacketModule` decodes S2C 0x039 into `WorldState.PostMapScheduler`; `ZoneTerrainRenderer` takes the requests for the loaded zone each frame (they wait while the zone loads; a zone change drops them) and `ZoneRoutinePlayer` plays every routine of that name, with the commands of the cutscene scene routines (`SceneEffectPlayer`, #192): 0x02 start, 0x1E kill, 0x3F replace, 0x03 / 0x73 start once / repeating, 0x5F stop. The portal's activation `1pa2` and end `1pak` play when a 0x039 names them; LandSandBoat sends neither (its Assault entry plays no map scheduler), so on an LSB server the portals show only their idle state. Since [#226](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/226) the event opcodes 0x2D / 0x60 sub 2 start and 0x51 ends a zone routine through the same queue (`IEventVmHost.StartZoneScheduler` / `StopZoneScheduler` → `PostMapScheduler`, `ZoneRoutinePlayer.Stop`), and 0x54 waits for the routine's length ([events/opcodes.md](../events/opcodes.md#0x2d-codemapschedulor)). The log says `Zone routine '1pa1' started (actors ...); N running in zone 72` / `stopped` (Graphics, Debug) for each request, `Map scheduler 'x' names no routine of zone N` for an unknown name, and `Zone scheduler 'sc00' on A / B in zone N: F frames` (EVENT, Debug) when an event starts one.
- **Never-expiring generators of on-demand routines ([#225](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/225), 2026-10-03, awaits the in-game test).** A non-auto-running generator whose particles never expire (max life span 0) used to become a static layer drawn from zone load whatever started it. Now it stays a static layer only when a zone-load routine schedules it (Bibiki Bay's sea stays as it was); one that only on-demand routines start is an idle emitter like the finite-life ones, so the portals' idle glows `g0b1` / `g0c1` appear with `1pa1` and go with `1pak`'s 0x1E kills, the second portal's `ooo1` / `r801`-`r808` with `2pb1` / `2pbk`; one that only a parent generator spawns as a child (Alzadaal's stage meshes `tw31`-`tw34`, which the pillars `tw21`-`tw24` leave behind on expiry: the child pass already drew them, so they were drawn twice) is its child layer alone. Of the 1,162 such generators in zones 0-299 (static-mesh link, outside weather directories), 543 in 26 zones are started only by on-demand routines (zone 170 has 210, Alzadaal 21) and 407 in 58 zones by no routine at all (92 of them under door directories); the last group keeps its static layer unless a parent spawns it. Checked offscreen (`ZoneRoutineRenderTests`): with the camera at the first portal, the frame's brightness is 107.6 before `1pa1`, 116.4 after it and 107.6 again after `1pak` (two more draws while lit).
- **Op 0x52 time gates and the other #81 items ([#81](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/81), 2026-10-03, awaits the in-game test).** *The auto-run rule* is #210's above (third-list op 0x01, or op 0x52); xim's single-routine-folder rule is not used (see the survey). *Op 0x52 (TimeBasedReplay)* is 24 bytes: after the delay and duration words, four dwords: the window's start and end on the Vana'diel clock, the longest and the shortest replay interval, all in Earth milliseconds of the Vana'diel clock (2,400 = one Vana'diel minute, 3,456,000 = one day). Read from all 485 commands in zones 0-299: 479 starts and 454 ends are whole minutes; 54 commands end before they start (the window wraps midnight: Carpenters' Landing's lightning `kmi1/s001` 23:15-03:00) and 134 ends and 127 starts lie past 24:00, taken modulo the day (`kmi1/s002` 28:30-33:00 = 04:30-09:00; `s003` 33:45-07:30 = 09:45 to 07:30 the next day, which explains the end field #81 could not place). The third dword takes 3,600-489,600 and often equals the window's length (Ronfaure's pigeons `hato/s002` 22:30-23:54 and 201,600 = 84 minutes; the ducks `kamo/s001` 10:30-11:24 and 129,600 = 54 minutes); the fourth only 3,600 / 7,200 / 10,800 / 18,000 / 21,600 / 36,000 and never exceeds the third (equal in the 12 daily triggers: Windurst's `kaza/tim1` 00:00-00:01 at 3,600 / 3,600, Aht Urhgan's fountain `funs/redf/on01` 03:01-09:00 at 7,200 / 7,200 and `off1` 09:01-09:03). GordianXI (`TimedReplayWindow`, `RoutineReplayTimer`, `EffectRoutineSpawn.Timer`, `ZoneParticleEmitter.AdvanceSchedule`): a routine with op 0x52 no longer loops at its own length; it plays when one of its windows opens on the zone's clock (`ZoneParticleFrame.DayFraction`, so an event's 0x77 clock lock moves it too) and again after each interval, drawn at random between the shortest and the longest, with a per-routine seed so every generator it starts fires together, and not at all outside its windows; the routines it starts (0x03 / 0x73) follow it. This holds in weather directories too: the 107 short timed weather routines (`kmi1` / `kmi2` lightning, `kam?`, `kami`) now fire on their own clocks under their weather instead of the random 4-15 s strike pause, which stays for the 57 short weather routines without op 0x52 (27 of them `kmi1`, `naga`, `_6ke` ...); the 125 long timed ones (`kamo`, `hato`, `tori`, `tobi`) replay in their windows instead of looping all day. Examples to check in game: Windurst Walls' `cyo/c101`-`c502` (10:30-11:51, 12:00-12:39, 13:30-14:09, 15:00-16:21, 16:30-17:09, 18:00-19:21, 19:30-20:09, 21:00-21:39, 22:30-23:09), Windurst Waters' `fra/s001` / `s002` (14:15-16:30, 19:30-22:30), Windurst Woods' `tobi/s001` (18:45-20:27), Port Windurst's ducks `mode/kamo` (12:00-13:30, 21:00-22:31, 00:45-02:15; their `kamo` generator attaches to a zone actor and is not drawn yet). *Inferred, not established:* that the fourth dword is the shortest interval of a random range (from the ordering and the equal pairs; xi-tools names only "start, end, interval"); whether retail plays the routine at once when a window opens. **Beyond xi-tools:** the units, the midnight wrap and modulo, the fourth dword, and the census. *Full-path keys:* generators, routines and keyframe curves are matched by their full directory path (`s_pa/effe/nami` and `s_pa/door/_030/nami` hold `yk**` generators of the same names, each with its own `lop0`-`lop2`; by the leaf name each generator took both schedules and emitted twice; 2,032 such spawns and 1,368 (leaf, curve id) collisions in #81's count), with curves resolved from the generator's directory up through its parents, then its weather directory, then anywhere. *Unknown ops* no reference names (zone sec2 `0x8F` / `0x4B`, sec3 `0x50` / `0x5A`, sec1 `0x15` / `0x17` / `0x5D`, routine `0x6F` / `0x70`, the last two in Alzadaal's `2pb1` / `2pbk` / `lig2`) stay stepped, not surveyed here.
- **Open.** Door entities' own effect routines (`door/_20t/open` → `door/_20t/door/stat`) do not play; the door work plays only their open / close transforms, and an event's 0x54 on a door's `open` / `clos` (46 of its 96 uses) ends at once ([#233](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/233)). Map schedulers and 0x2D that name an entity are played on the zone's routine of that name without the entity: of the 5,616 generator starts in on-demand routines, 1,037 name generators attached to an actor (416 `ZoneActor0xA`, 380 `TargetActor`, 150 `TargetActorSourceFacing`, 47 `SourceActor`, 43 `ZoneActor0xB/C`, 1 `SourceActorWeapon`), which the zone loader does not place at all (Port Windurst's ducks among them; [#232](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/232)). 476 of the 1,500 0x2D uses name a routine the zone DAT lacks (zone 14's `sc11` / `kaks` / `4aks`...), presumably in a resource not read yet ([#233](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/233)).

## Weighted meshes (0x25)

- **Step: Weighted Meshes (#204, 2026-10-02):** generators linked to a Section 0x25 weighted mesh (linked data type 0x1D) run as emitters in zones, actor and scene DATs. `WeightedMeshDecoder` reads the header (`u16 1`; flags: low nibble the target count, 0x80 alpha discard; an offset extension byte times 0x10000 added to the index and UV offsets; position count, normal count, index offset, triangle count, colour offset, UV offset; a 16-byte texture name), then at +0x20 each morph target's positions (3 x f32) followed by its normals packed 10:10:10 (signed X, Y, Z from bit 0; 0x20000000 = -Z, the flat masks' normal), then per triangle-list vertex a BGRA colour (doubled like 0x1F's in zone DATs only, #208) and a UV pair, and the index streams: every vertex's u16 position index, then every vertex's u16 normal index. Checked on all 680 retail 0x25 sections: 659 fill exactly to the colour offset, the rest carry extra data before it (`skb1`, the door meshes) or end two normals short of it (`fk01`); 468 have 2 targets, 138 three, 45 four, 27 five. Which index stream is which was settled on 51402's `mb`: the first tiles the 1 x 1 card with no degenerate triangle (area 1.000), the second gives five. Header referenced from xi-model-viewer (`ui/js/dat/inspect.js` `parseInspectWeightedMesh`).
- Each particle keeps five weights (`ZoneParticle.MeshWeights`): progress updaters 0x1E-0x22 set weights 0-4 from their curves, 0x6E sets weight 0 from the camera distance band (near..far, as 0x48) and weight 1 to its complement; a particle takes its weights at birth too (it is drawn the frame it is born). A generator with no weight updater draws its first target. The blend is a weighted sum normalized to 1 (weights summing to 0 draw the first target): Alzadaal's swimming fish (`fsk1`, curves `mf00`-`mf03`, 15 cycles a life) sum to 1.74 mid-stroke and swelled with a plain sum in the in-game test (2026-10-02); negative weights stay (51328's `uw` runs from 1.5 / -0.5 to 0 / 1). `ZoneTerrainRenderer` blends each particle's mesh into a dynamic vertex buffer before its draw (`WeightedMesh.Blend`, display axes) and culls on twice the farthest target.
- Curves whose first segment is a step authored 1e-7 wide (51402's `m001`: 0, then 0.96) take the stepped value at progress 0, so 324's mask is born closed rather than collapsed (`KeyFrameCurve.Evaluate`).
- Seen offscreen: 324's blink opens as an eye (lids curving to points, `SceneEffectRenderTests`); its shape and opening times match the maintainer's retail recording (a slit from about 0.5 s after `open`, fully open by about 2.3 s, about 2.5 times as wide as tall). Alzadaal's tentacles (`tako`, 1,155 vertices) wrap its pillars. A walk of zones 0-299 found weighted-mesh layers in about 50 zones (fish `ayuc` / `arok`, birds `tobi` / `hato` / `wasi`, geysers `mgy?`, butterflies, the Riverne warp lights `1wps`), now drawn. The blink's black cards `bk01` / `md00` stayed opaque until about frame 125, where retail shows the scene dimly inside the slit from about frame 30: their alpha was doubled twice, fixed by [Particle alpha scale](#particle-alpha-scale-208) ([#208](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/208)).

## Particle alpha scale (#208)

- **Step: Half-scale particle alpha (#208, 2026-10-03, awaits the in-game test):** the particle fragment shader (`ZoneShaders.FragmentShaderWeatherSkyGlsl`, the client's two modulate-2x stages after xi-model-viewer `particleDrawer.js`) gives alpha = 4 x (2 x vertex.a x texture.a) x factor.a, built for half-scale inputs: vertex 0x80, texel 0x80 and generator colour 0x80 give alpha 1 and the texel's own colour. Two inputs arrived doubled:
  - *Paletted texture alpha.* `TextureDecoder` doubles paletted alpha (0x80 -> 0xFF) for the terrain and model shaders, but not DXT alpha. FFXI authors both at half scale: a walk of zones 0-299 (2026-10-03) found 10,169 of 12,339 DXT3 textures peaking at 0x88 and 1,347 of 2,171 paletted ones at 0x80 (at least 81 paletted ones peak at 0xFF). Of the 2,712 resolved texture references from zone particle meshes and sprite sheets (counted once per zone), 1,770 are DXT3 and 942 paletted. The decoder is left as it is (terrain cutouts and model alpha depend on it); the particle shader halves a paletted texel's alpha back instead (texture alpha mode 2 in `SunDirection.w`, from `DecodedTexture.AlphaDoubled`, `ZoneTerrainRenderer.ParticleTextureAlphaMode`), so paletted and DXT3 particles now get the same scale. Paletted textures authored above 0x80 lose their top range (the decoder clamps at 0xFF before the halving).
  - *Model and scene DAT vertex colours.* `ParticleMeshDecoder` and `WeightedMeshDecoder` doubled vertex colours for every DAT. Only zone DATs keep the doubling now (`zoneResource: !actorEffects` in `ZoneDataLoader`); model DATs (actor effects) and cutscene scene DATs use their colours as authored, as the client does for the shared effects DAT (xi-model-viewer `dat/sections.js` `makeParsers`: only zone resources get the x2 vertex-colour scale). The scene files of Port Jeuno 324 (51327, 51328, 51402, 70443) author their mesh colours at 0x80 (neutral), so the doubling had drawn them at twice the texel colour.
  - *Measured:* the maintainer's retail recording of Port Jeuno 324 shows the scene at about 20-25% brightness inside the blink's slit 88 frames after `open` (`bk01` colour alpha 0.28, `md00` 0.21), which 2 x the colour alpha gives (0.44 x 0.58 = 26% let through); the card `bk00` (0x80) is fully black. Both fixes are needed for that: the paletted texture `nb` (51402, type 0xB1) and vertex colour 80 80 80 80 made the cards 8 x their colour alpha, either fix alone 4 x (still opaque at frame 88). Offscreen (`SceneEffectRenderTests.BlinkCards_LetTheSceneThroughDimlyMidway`, Bibiki Bay behind the cards) the centre now keeps 25.0% of the scene at frame 88 (0.0% before) and `bk00` stays black.
  - *Affected:* zone particles with paletted textures (alpha halved where it did not saturate; their colour is unchanged), e.g. the sun lens flares `lf01`-`lf03` and `suny`'s glare texture in Bibiki Bay and Carpenters' Landing (not compared with retail before). The calibrated zone effects use DXT3 textures and are unchanged: Bibiki Bay's sea, surf and glints (`umi1`, `shn1`, `yuh1`...), Carpenters' Landing's rain (`rain_b01`). Model and scene DAT effects drawn with 0x1F / 0x25 meshes (actor effects such as the Home Point crystal's, cutscene effects) draw at half the colour and half the alpha of before where they did not saturate, and half the alpha again with a paletted texture (324's sparkles, 70443's `mmr_ligt*`); sprite-sheet (0x21) colours were never doubled, so those change only with a paletted texture. Open: recheck 324's explosion, sparkles and white flash and a zone's paletted particles against retail.

## Closed questions

- Closed: point lights on entities (not needed). Repeat-expiration (`0x05`) generators do not accumulate: all 6,085 in the zone DATs are continuous singletons. Actor-attached weather effects (e.g. `weat/clod/tobi` birds) moved to Phase 5C model-embedded effect routines. Model DATs' own generators run on their actors through the same emitters (see [entities-and-animation.md](../world/entities-and-animation.md), #9), and so do the generators of cutscene scene files, camera-following ones included (`SceneEffectPlayer`, [events/vm.md](../events/vm.md#cutscene-schedulers), #192). Generators drawing a weighted mesh (Section 0x25) are drawn since #204 ([Weighted meshes](#weighted-meshes-0x25)).
