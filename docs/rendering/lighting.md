# Terrain, Actor & Point Lighting

> How Section 0x2F environment colours, point lights and sub-environments light terrain and actors. Calibrations were made against Windower captures of the legacy client. Status and open work: [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) and GitHub Issues.

## Terrain lighting from 0x2F (chunk 1)

- **Chunk 1: Sand & Terrain Lighting Balance:** Calibrated diffuse and ambient lighting coefficients (`0.5 * amb + 0.5 * df0`) in terrain shaders to prevent overexposure of light-colored ground/sand while maintaining shadow depth. *Superseded (2026-09-24, legacy parity):* terrain/entity lighting is now `clamp(amb + sun + moon)` with 0x2F colors converted as the legacy client does (ambient `/510` + dark bias, clamp 0.5; sun/moon `* diffuseMult` + dark bias), and a moonlight term opposite the sun. *Superseded again (2026-09-26):* the 0x2F bytes now feed the lights as is, ambient = byte/255 and sun/moon = byte/255 x `light_power`, with no dark lift, no halving and no clamp before the lit vertex result (`ZoneEnvironmentSettings.AmbientToLight`/`DiffuseToLight`). Calibrated on a Windower capture of Bibiki Bay at 15:58 (Windower (642.7, 879.3, -20), `suny`): flat sunlit sand measured (182,105,77) in retail against (137,93,66) from the halved ambient; the byte/255 conversion renders it at (184,103,74) and brings the east-facing cliffs from (60,43,40) to (73,52,47) against retail's (76,50,47). Shadows were ~40% too dark before, which read as an over-strong sun. Particle daylight tints clamp at 1 (`StrongestLight`). Open: verify noon against Windower (the sand now saturates to near-white there, as the unclamped 1.5x sun plus a 0.7 ambient predict).

## Point lights

- *Chunk 2: Point lights (~15.6k generators).* The ZoneDef light table (header `+0x18`, 256 x `0x4C`, generator FourCC at `+0`) binds each `0x47` point-light generator to a slot by its own FourCC, and each placement's four 1-based references at `+0x54` are the only lights that shine on it (binding from xi-tools `docs/zone/format.md`). Light generators run through the emitter (`0x58` range / theta / multipliers `2^x`, clock theta `0x49`, progress `0x5B`-`0x5E`, parent `0x7E`/`0x7F`); life-0 generators (and 1-frame point lights) now emit once and live forever. Terrain shaders add `vColor x max(N.L, 0) x 2 x color x theta x (1 - d/range)^2` per referenced light inside the ambient + sun + moon clamp. Southern San d'Oria's lamps turn on at ~19:00 via their `pttm` theta curve, and the falloff/power were calibrated against Windower captures there (22:03 door lamps, 20:30 wall lamp).

## Indoor sub-environments and actor lighting

- *Indoor sub-environment lighting & point-light power (2026-09-25):* placements linked to a sub-environment (ZoneDef +0x4C, e.g. Metalworks' 627 `ev01` / 118 `ev02` groups) are lit by that environment's own 0x2F keyframes (per-environment scene uniform sets; indoor light direction decoded from the moon slot's signed bytes) instead of the outdoor weather, which had lit Metalworks' interiors with noon daylight. Point lights saturate per channel: colour = min(half-range colour x2 x power, 1) x 0.75, so power decides how pale a light is rather than scaling it (Metalworks' always-on power 12-40 orange lights wash to a pale yellow, San d'Oria's power-2 gold lamps stay gold); calibrated against Windower captures of both zones, where linear power made Metalworks 6-20x too bright and orange (the client's exact light pipeline is not decoded). Actors follow the same rule (2026-09-25): each entity probes the floor under its feet (`ZoneRaycaster.FindFloor`, re-cast after half a yalm of movement) and, on a floor linked to a sub-environment, takes that environment's lights, so characters in Bibiki Bay's entrance cave (`ev01`: sun/ambient ~0.27/0.31, blue-tinted) no longer glow with the outdoor noon light. Actors are lit with the 0x2F model lighting block (sun, moon, ambient) at twice the 0x80-neutral strength (`ActorLighting`, scale 2: character meshes carry no vertex colours): Windower captures in Bibiki Bay showed retail characters 2.6-3x brighter at night outdoors (22:46) and 2-2.5x in the entrance cave, matching the prediction; the summed actor light is capped at 0.75 (1.5x the texel, the pre-doubling noon peak) because at noon the doubled lights flattened skin tones to twice the texel; checked in-game across the F10 presets (day, dusk, night, overcast). Open: the cap is an estimate; compare characters at noon against a Windower capture of the same spot.
