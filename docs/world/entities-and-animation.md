# Entity Models & Animation

> Modular character assembly, NPC/monster models, skeletal animation and combat action playback (Phases 5C, 5D and 5D.1). Status and open work: [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) and GitHub Issues.

## Models and equipment (Phase 5C)

- Dynamic character mesh decoder stitching Race + Face + 5 Armor Slots (Head, Body, Hands, Legs, Feet) + Weapons from distinct DATs (`CharacterSlot`: Face=0, Head=1, Body=2, Hands=3, Legs=4, Feet=5, Main=6, Sub=7, Ranged=8; playable `CharacterRace`: Hume M/F 1/2, Elvaan M/F 3/4, Taru M/F 5/6, Mithra 7, Galka 8).
- Bind-pose entity rendering at live `WorldEntity` coordinates
- **Gear occlusion (#88).** Worn gear hides the body pieces it covers, as in retail.
  - Every 0x2A mesh declares an `OccludeType` (header byte 3): what it hides on other pieces. Every piece carries a `DisplayType`: byte 13 of the 0x8010 render-properties block in force when the piece is read, 0 before any.
  - `AssembleCharacter` builds the union of occludeTypes across the worn set, and `GearOcclusion` drops each piece it hides:

    | displayType | Piece | Hidden by |
    |---|---|---|
    | 1 | hair | `0x02`-`0x06` |
    | 2, 3 | hair | `0x04`-`0x06` |
    | 4 | face | `0x05` |
    | 5 | wrist | `0x12` |
    | 6 | pants | `0x32` |
    | 7 | shins | `0x22` |

    `0x11`/`0x21`/`0x31` are body/legs/feet self-markers that hide nothing. Models are cached per grap table, so an equipment change reassembles with the new union.
  - The ranged weapon is part of the model (weapon slot 2) but hidden until a ranged attack shows it; its occludeType counts either way. See *Ranged attacks and weapon visibility* below (#158).
  - Checked on retail Hume Male DATs (`GearOcclusionRealDataTests`, 2026-09-30): full helm head 22 (`0x05`) with sleeved body 3 (`0x12`) hides 14 face-DAT pieces (displayTypes 1, 3, 4) and 2 hand pieces (5), 2374 to 1952 triangles. This matches xi-tools `xi gear pose`.
  - Fixed NPC models get the same rule (#181): `LoadMonsterModel` takes the union over the model DAT's own meshes. Humanoid NPCs such as Apururu (model 164: hat `0x04`, face mesh with hair displayTypes 1 and 3; 2246 to 1848 triangles) bundle gear meshes in one DAT. A scan of models 1-3999 (2026-10-01) found 300+ models with pieces to hide, all humanoid NPCs; monster DATs hide nothing.
  - Rule and byte positions referenced from xi-tools `docs/gear/pose.md` ("Hidden pieces", after xim `ActorModel.isOccluded`).
- **Where a PC's look comes from.** Other players: the grap id table in S2C `0x00D` (only when the Model send flag is set). Your own character: S2C `0x00A` at zone-in, then S2C `0x051` `GRAP_LIST`, which LSB sends at login, game-ok, on every visible equipment change and on lockstyle. `SessionNetworkManager` keeps the latest table and reapplies it to the local entity. `ResourceManager.TryLoadEntityModel` caches one assembled `EntityModel` per race, face and full grap table. `EntityRenderer` caches GPU buffers per `EntityModel` instance, not by model name: a PC model is named `{race}_Face{n}` whatever it wears, so a name key made same-race, same-face characters share the first one's gear (#153). When `ResourceManager.ClearCache` runs it bumps `CacheGeneration`, and `EntityRenderer` then frees its GPU copies of the dropped models.
- NPC, Monster, and Trust model rendering from DAT resource caches (monsters/NPC models resolve by `CharacterEquipmentResolver.GetMonsterFileId`: model + 1300 below 1500, + 50295 below 3000, + 96907 below 3193, no model for 3193-3499 (retail draws nothing), + 98239 from 3500. **Differs from xi-model-viewer** (+ 98546 from 3193), per the 2026-10-17 costume captures, #83; see [dat-files.md](dat-files.md#entity-models-and-motions)).
- NPC-only child races (look race 29 Mithra kitten, 30 girl, 31 boy; 115 server NPCs such as Southern San d'Oria's Authere and Blendare): skeletons `ROM/61/110`, `ROM/61/58`, `ROM/61/85` carry their own idle / walk / run clips, and each outfit slot resolves to `slotBase + modelId` (0-19 Hume, 20+ Elvaan variants).
- [ ] **Actor status visuals:** effects attached to characters by status effects (e.g. the Refresh/Regen afterglow on the characters in the Bibiki Bay captures). Can reuse the actor effect runtime below.
- **Model-embedded effects (#9):** a model DAT's Section 0x05 generators run on its actor. `ActorEffectLoader` parses the DAT with the zone effect parser in actor mode: generators of every attach type except sun/moon, all as emitters, plus the DAT's Section 0x07 routines by name. `ResourceManager.GetActorEffects(modelId)` caches the result per model and skips DATs without generators. `ZoneTerrainRenderer` runs one `ActorEffectInstance` per spawned NPC or monster with effects, simulated in the actor's model space (raw DAT axes, camera taken into that space). The instance's auto-running generators emit from spawn, and `Play(routine)` starts the others. Particles are drawn in the zone effect pass, placed by the actor's model transform from `EntityRenderer.ActorAnchors`, at the skeleton joint reference the generator names: attach bits 4-9 for source-side types, bits 10-15 for target-side ones (xi-model-viewer `getActorAttachPosition`).
  - Effect-only models now draw. The Home Point crystal (model 51, `ROM/3/25.DAT`) shows its eight idle layers (crystal `bnd0`, aura shells, cross planes, ground rings, sparkle cards); the skeleton mesh is a 3 mm placeholder (xi-tools `docs/dats/ROM_3_25.md`). Verified offscreen in Southern San d'Oria (2026-09-28).
  - Scope from a scan of model ids 1-3192 (file id = model + 1300 / + 50295 / + 96907): 648 models carry drawable generators, 379 of them auto-running (idle) ones, e.g. elementals 8-15, Home Points 51/55/57, and portals.
  - **Scheduler packets ([#311](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/311), 2026-10-18, awaits the in-game test).** `SchedulerPlayback` plays S2C 0x038 and 0x03A as they arrive. *0x038*: the caster plays the named routine of its own motion DATs as an event gesture does (`ActionMotion.EventMotion`, when it has one), and the viewport plays the routine's effects on it (`WorldState.PostActorEffect` → `ZoneTerrainRenderer.PlayScheduledActorEffects`): the model's own generators of that routine (`ActorEffectInstance.Play`: the Home Point's `bind`), and the shared `ROM/0/0` routine of that name, whose colour commands fade the entity (`syst/acti/kesu`, LandSandBoat's despawn fade, is op 0x29 to alpha 0 over 90 frames; see *Routine colour* below) and whose actor links play the actor's own routines (`syst/acti/hitl`, the fishing sweat: 0x57 `eflg`, `selg`). *0x03A*: `fileNum` is read as the animation number of the family its `type` names, and the presentation file plays on the caster toward the target like an action's: type 0 a spell (2800 + n), 2 a job ability (4412 + n), 6 a weapon skill (the caster's race bank), 9 a monster skill; its `main` moves the body (`ActionRequest.TargetId` carries the target for a request without hits) and the viewport plays its effects once. The other types (1 item, 3 / 4 / 5 / 8 / 10-12 events, banners, warps: LandSandBoat's hearts / notes / lightbulb 250-252 with type 4) are logged and not played, their files not known. *Inference*: that types 0 / 2 / 6 / 9 use the action numbering (LandSandBoat sends `xi.magic.spell.WARP` and 122 with type 0, `imperial_agent_rescue.lua`, `Archaic_Gear.lua`). (A cross-zone Home Point warp currently leaves the player stuck in an event: #122.) Target-side generators (the `bind` flash on the player's joints) ride the effect's own actor until actions bind a target.
  - **Routine colour (#206, #311).** Routine op 0x29 moves the colour an actor is drawn with toward a B G R A colour over the command's duration (0x80 = as drawn, alpha 0 = invisible, FF = twice as bright), op 0x2E holds the change for its duration and then the actor is drawn as is (`WorldEntity.ActorColor`, `ActorColorTrack`; `SceneCommandKind.ActorColor` / `ActorHold`). Played from 0x038's shared routines and from event scene routines (`EventDialogController.StartSceneTask`; reset when the event ends). The entity renderer draws the alpha through the event fade path (the lower of it and 0x6C's `EventAlpha`); the colour (the warps' white wash) is read but not drawn yet. Evidence: `kesu` above; the battlefield warp `wp00` (file 5013) goes to FF FF FF 80 over frames 0-60 and to 80 80 80 00 over 90-150 under a 150-frame 0x2E, `wp01` (5014) starts at 80 80 80 00, goes to FF FF FF 80 over 20-60 and back to 80 80 80 80 over 60-120 under a 120-frame 0x2E; the maintainer's Shooting Fish BCNM capture (Horlais Peak, 2026-10-17) shows the player turning white and translucent into the warp glow, and later reappearing white from nothing and turning solid. *Inference*: no reference names either op (#206's other unknown ops stay stepped).
  - **State-driven models ([#257](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/257), 2026-10-18, awaits the in-game test).** In a model DAT with the state routines `ids0`, `ids1`..., auto-running generators that a routine starts wait for that routine (`ActorEffectSet.StateTriggered`, `ZoneParticleEmitter.StartsOnTrigger`) instead of emitting from spawn. Synthesis Focuser II (model 2335, file 52630) flags all fifteen of its orb generators `md_s/{b,y,r}{gp0,gp1,gp2,fl0,cf0}` auto-running; its `ids1` / `ids2` / `ids3` start one colour each (`bslp` blue, `yslp` yellow, `rslp` red) and `ids0` kills them all (`alkl`), so all three colours showed at once on an idle focuser. The maintainer's retail capture of an idle furnace (Aht Urhgan Whitegate, 2026-10-17) shows no effect, in use only bursts in response to actions. A scan of models 1-3499 (2026-10-18): 437 models have auto-running drawn generators, 412 of them started by a routine too, 153 of those with `ids0`; the rest (the Home Point's idle `bind` layers, which its `aper` also starts; the elementals' `aper`) keep emitting from spawn. **Open:** the models' `init` links a state routine (`mse0`-`mse3`) that picks `ids?` / `sp??` with ops 0x69 / 0x6B / 0x64 / 0x67 / 0x6A on two actor fields (+0x3A / +0x3C, read as an expression VM: field, constant, compare 0x0C; not decoded), so the in-use states (the furnace lid closing, the focuser's colours, the furnace's element bursts) do not play yet. *Inference* from the data.
    - *Rest stance (round 1 E7, 2026-10-19).* The state routines set the stance with op 0x8C (`ids0` 0 `idl`, `ids1` 1 `1dl`, `ids2` 2 `2dl` with the orb loop `s112`, `ids3` 3 `3dl` with `s013`) and show or hide weapon-slot meshes with op 0xA3 (u32 flag at +8, u16 slot at +0x0C, as 0x75; the furnace's `wep0`, `wep3`-`wep7`). - **Player size (#33 round 2).** A player entity's `GraphSize` (S2C 0x00D flags, 0 small / 1 medium / 2 large) scales its whole model uniformly about its feet (`PlayerSizeScale`: 0.94 / 1.0 / 1.06, guessed, [calibrations](../reference/calibrations.md#player-size)); the overhead point (name plate, target cursor) follows. NPCs and monsters are not scaled. The lobby preview uses the same scale for the character's or the creation's size.

### Mounts and chocobos (#87)

Mounted players ([#87](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/87), 2026-10-20, awaits the in-game test). Evidence: the maintainer's retail capture `Mounts.mp4` (2026-10-16/17: a Galka and a Tarutaru on a raptor, a Hume on a personal chocobo) and the retail DATs.

- **State (`MountAppearance`, `WorldEntity.Mount`).** Server status 5 rides a chocobo, 85 another mount; anything else is on foot, whatever mount number the server keeps sending (LandSandBoat "almost always" sets it). The wire number (0x00D `Flags6.MountIndex`, 0x037 `mount_id`) is the client's mount id (the mount list's order: 0 Chocobo, 1 Raptor ... 33 Noble Chocobo, 38 Bubble Crab) plus 1: LandSandBoat adds 1 to the id C2S 0x01A sends (`0x01a_action.cpp`), and its `xi.mount` enum carries the extra `QUEST_RAPTOR = 1` that "now appears as a plain chocobo". Other players' mounts come from 0x00D (General updates), the local player's from 0x037 (`LocalPlayerState.Mount`, copied onto its entity by `EntityPacketModule.HandleCharStatus` and every locomotion tick). `ChocoboIndex` (1 rental, 2 personal) and `CustomProperties[0]` (a personal chocobo's look) are kept. *Inference*: the +1 rule (no reference states the client's side; it is the only reading under which LandSandBoat's raptor is a raptor).
- **Models (`MountModels`, `ResourceManager.TryLoadMountModel`).** A mount is its own model DAT at file id 0x019131 + id (xi-tools `docs/mounts/mechanism.md`): 102706 the blue-and-white raptor of the capture (texture `wy`), 102707 the tiger, 102735 the red raptor. The chocobo and Noble Chocobo are the chocobo race (`ROM/169/11`) with the saddled body, feet, head and tail of their colour ([dat-files.md](dat-files.md#entity-models-and-motions)); a personal chocobo takes its colour from `CustomProperties` bits 9-11 and, *inference*, the second file of the head / feet / tail pair for its large beak, large talons and full tail (bits 0, 3, 6). **Differs from xi-tools:** 102705 (id 0) is byte-identical to the raptor, not a chocobo. The mount's idle `chi` is filed as `idl` so the stance resolver plays it at rest.
- **Seating, from the data (`MountSeating`, round 5, 2026-10-23).** Every mount's size, rider pose and seat come from its own model DAT and the mount rider packs; nothing is tuned per mount. Layout in [dat-files.md](dat-files.md#entity-models-and-motions). **Beyond xi-tools / xi-model-viewer / XiPackets** except the Info "Scale" name.
  - *On the chocobo.* The rider plays its race's chocobo riding pack (`ROM/90/14` for Hume male, every 6 files per race, waist part 2 on): `chi` idle, `wlk`, `run`, `mvb`, and the routines `chor` / `coff` / `cdam`. Each pack is authored on a chocobo scaled to its race (the hips stay 0.34 x s over the chocobo's reference 1 through `chi`, `run` and `wlk`), with s = the pack's hip height over the Hume male's 1.7802: Hume female 0.995, Elvaan male 1.233, Elvaan female 1.216, Tarutaru 0.955, Mithra 0.982, Galka 1.298. The chocobo is drawn at s (`MountModels.MountScale`) and the pack fits it as authored. The chocobo race has no per-race data of its own.
  - *Size on other mounts.* Info (0x45 `info`) bytes 10-12 by the rider's build: 10 Hume, Mithra and Tarutaru, 11 Elvaan, 12 Galka (13 is not used by a playable race). Examples:

    | Mount | Bytes 10-13 |
    |---|---|
    | raptor | 85, 100, 115, 140 |
    | Fenrir | 60, 60, 60, 100 |
    | Magic Pot | 50, 50, 50, 110 |
    | Beetle | 130, 130, 130, 140 |

    The raptor's values match image-12.png's per-race fits: 0.84-0.86 for Hume, Mithra and Tarutaru, 1.04 for Elvaan, 1.11 for Galka. Fenrir is the same size under every race, as image-13.png's five Fenrirs are; rounds 2-4 scaled it by the race, too big under the Galka. xi-model-viewer names byte 10 "Scale".
  - *Pose.* The second Info section `moun`, bytes 8-15, holds one pose per race in the retail race order. The rider plays that pose from its race's mount rider pack: `ROM/351/61` + 3 per race (Hume male, Hume female, Elvaan male, Elvaan female, Tarutaru, Mithra, Galka), with the clips `{n}hi` idle, `{n}lk`, `{n}un`, `{n}vb`. All four clips of a pose are the same length, so the rider holds the pose and the mount carries it. Poses (CPU-skinned silhouettes, 2026-10-23):
    - 6 straddle: identical to the chocobo pack's `chi`; the raptor, Fenrir, the Beetle and most others.
    - 5 stand: the Magic Pot (every race, as retail's image-26.png) and Levitus.
    - 3 sit as on a chair: the Spectral Chair, Moogle, Iron Giant, the Mithra and Galka on a Bomb.
    - 2 sit on the floor: Morbol, Goobbue.
    - 1 kneel, 4 lie prone, 7 crouch.
  - *Seat.* Skeleton reference 48 + the race's index (r48 Hume male ... r55 Galka) is where the rider's hips go, in the mount's current pose, so the rider bobs with it, idle included (`MountModels.RiderHips`, `EntityRenderer.PrepareMount`). The references sit:
    - just over the raptor's back, at one height for every race (1.43-1.47, the Tarutaru's 0.18 further forward);
    - on Fenrir's mid-back, 0.5-0.75 behind its hip joint; round 3 had moved Fenrir's seat 0.6 back by hand, and that tune is dropped;
    - over the Magic Pot's lid, each race at its standing hip height (Tarutaru 5.89, Hume 7.09, Elvaan 7.41 model units), so every race stands on the lid.
  - *Checked offscreen* (2026-10-23) for Galka, Elvaan male, Hume female, Hume male, Mithra and Tarutaru on the raptor, Fenrir, Magic Pot, Beetle, Spectral Chair, Moogle, Morbol, Goobbue, Adamantoise and Bomb. Earlier rounds checked the chocobo.
  - *Not decoded:* `moun` bytes 0-7 (small signed per-race values: the tiger 0x40 for Hume female, Elvaan female and Mithra; the morbol -20 for most) and Info byte 13; the mount packs' `chi` set (pose 0) and the poses' own routines are not used.
- **Drawing (`EntityRenderer.Mounts.cs`).** The mount is posed first (`PrepareMount`); the rider keeps its GPU mesh, animates with the rider model and is drawn at its seat; the mount is drawn after it at the entity's origin and heading, scaled, with its own `WorldEntity.MountAnimation` (the rider's movement category: idle, walk, run, move back; strafes run), its own joint palette (key: server id with the top bit set), event fades, a shadow and its blur aura (Section 0x5E: of the retail mounts only Fenrir, id 9, carries one; round 1 note, 2026-10-21). The name plate is the rider's own overhead point (reference 2, 2.0 yalms for a Hume) carried with the rider: on a straddle raised by half the seat's height (round 2 H12: just over the head was too low against image-9.png; round 3 H17: the full seat height a little too high), in the other poses over the rider's origin on its support ([calibrations](../reference/calibrations.md#movement-collision-and-camera)).
- **Speed.** Mounted, the local player runs at twice the server's speed (LandSandBoat sends `MOUNT_SPEED` 80 halved; the capture measures a steady 7.8-8.2 yalms/s on a chocobo and a raptor, no ramp), walks at half that, and the classifier's walk / run split doubles with it.
- **Not done:** the mount-up flourish (`chor`: rider and chocobo play `chh`, 168 ticks) and the dismount (`coff`: the rider's `ch1` / `ch2`, the chocobo or raptor running off; the capture shows the rider standing beside the mount afterwards), which need the mount to outlive the status change; the chocobo's armour (`ROM/188/69`) and other raised-chocobo parts; `ChocoboIndex` above 3 "changes the manner in which the player is seated" (XiPackets) and is not read; mounted bump collision.

### Blur aura (#82)

- **Data.** Section 0x5E (`BlurAura.Decode`, read for every model by `EntityModelLoader.AssembleModel`) is on 57 retail models: the avatars (16-23, 25, 28, 30), the Prime avatars (791-798) and others (772, 779, 780, 978, 995, 1071-1073, 1145, 1160, 1206, 1266, 1308, 1477, 1522, 2205, 2226, 2227, 2325, 2401-2468, 2611-2680, 2938, 2939, 3081-3085). Layout per xi-tools (`src/xi/entity/xi_blur.py`): layers, shimmer frequency, falloff, two base colours, inner radius, then seven rings (RGB, alpha, radius) and a ring at radius 0. Garuda (21): grey rings (neutral), alpha 10-12, radii 60-130, base colour 192; Fenrir (17): cyan (128, 255, 255) rings, alpha 16, all at radius 110.
- **Pass.** After all actors, each actor with an aura draws its skinned body once per ring as a shell pushed out along its normals (`BlurAuraShaders`, `EntityRenderer.BlurAura.cs`, D3D11 and Vulkan): the colour is the body's texel times the ring colour and the base colour (both modulate-2x, so 0x80 is neutral), weighted by a rim term (zero face-on, full edge-on), added to the frame with depth test and no depth write. Each ring drifts a few pixels on screen at the shimmer frequency, which gives the faint offset ghost copies. So Garuda gets a whitish halo on her body and a pale green one past her wings, Fenrir a thin cyan rim, as in the maintainer's retail capture `SMN_Avatars.mp4` (2026-10-17, night: Garuda at 18 s, Fenrir at 46 s). No disc: the glow follows the silhouette. Faded or event-hidden actors draw no aura; static (unskinned) models with an aura are not handled.
- **Calibration** (by eye, [calibrations](../reference/calibrations.md#lighting-sky-and-particles)): width = radius x 0.00015 yalm, strength = alpha / 96 per ring, rim exponent = falloff / 60, drift up to 0.0012 NDC at 11 layers, phase speed 0.6 rad/s per frequency unit. Round 1 in game (TESTING-2026-10-18, F9): retail's glow is much closer to the body, "basically just the edges of their model, like an outline"; the first values (width x 0.0005, rim / 120, drift 0.0035) drew a halo. The client's own use of the fields is not decoded.
- **Not reproduced:** retail also blurs and slightly darkens the background inside the halo (the trees behind Garuda lose detail). That needs the scene behind the actor as a texture (a scene copy before the aura pass); open on [#82](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/82).

## Skeletal animation (Phase 5D)

- FFXI bone hierarchy & joint matrix tree parser (Section `0x2B` `SkeletonAnimationDecoder`, GPU joint-palette skinning in `EntityRenderer`/`ZoneShaders`)
- Quaternion normalized-lerp (NLERP, matching documented retail behavior) rotation & translation keyframe interpolation (`AnimationClip.TrySample`, `SkeletonPoseEvaluator.EvaluatePose`)
- **Scale keys hide parts ([#76](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/76)).** `SkeletonAnimationDecoder` keeps every scale key as stored, zero and near-zero included; only a non-finite component becomes 1. It used to turn keys of 1e-4 or less into 1. Scale accumulates down the hierarchy and skinning (CPU `SkinVertex`, GPU palette `uScale`) multiplies the bind-space position by it, so a joint at scale 0 collapses its own geometry and its children's onto the joint. Nothing divides by the scale: normals are only rotated, bounds come from the bind pose, joint-reference offsets (`ActorEffectInstance.ResolveJointReference`) multiply, and blends lerp the scale. xi-model-viewer (`ui/js/dat.js`, `pose.js`) also keeps the raw values.
  - *Evidence* (scan of model ids 1-3999, 2026-10-03): 295 of 2,788 model DATs with clips hide geometry this way in some clip; by stem `idl` 115 clips, `run` 103, `wlk` 101, `btl` 81, `cor` 77, `ded` 76. They hide weapons and props that other motions show: the Qiqirn model 1735 (`ROM/258/91`) holds its bow on joints 104 and 107 at scale 0 through `idl`, `wlk`, `run`, `btl` (at scale 1 the bow lay at its feet), the samurai model 1182 (`ROM/151/126`) a second blade on joints 10, 89, 91, the Trust Najelith (model 3033) her bow and an arrow (at scale 1 they hung below her feet). Checked by CPU-skinning both offscreen (`ZeroScaleStance_HidesThePart`). Model 37 (`ROM/3/11`) holds joint 78 at (0, 0, 0) through `idl1`, but no mesh of that DAT is bound there. The fishing rod clips (xi-tools `docs/anim/fishing.md`) use 1e-5 to keep the rod hidden until the cast.
  - *Player characters.* The race packs hold one joint and its four children at scale 0 in their stances (`idl1`, `wlk1`, `run1`, `std1`, `ded1`...): Hume male 89, Hume female 78, Elvaan male 72, Elvaan female 26, Tarutaru 45, Mithra 89; Galka none. Of the gear DATs only ranged weapons bind there (133 of 254 ranged models per race), hidden while stowed and carried into the hand by the ranged clips (#158), plus five legs models (Elvaan female 245 and 580, Mithra 405, 448, 496) with a few vertices on that chain; a CPU-skin of Mithra legs 405 and Elvaan female legs 245 shows no visible change.
- Full-body locomotion & battle motion packs enabled by default (`EnableSpeculativeMotionPacks = true`). Resolved origin blob collapse root cause (additive translation kinematics $t = t_{\text{bind}} + \Delta t$, Hamilton rotation composition, normalized multi-rate track sampling phase alignment, and folder-carry legacy battle pack file number resolution). Resting locomotion is preserved against battle locomotion clobbering, and in-game renderer supports battle stance (`"btl"`) and death (`"ded"`).
- Authentic entity animation state & motion resolution: monster suffix resolution (`idl0`, `wlk0`, `run0`), packet `MovTime` parsing ensuring stationary NPCs idle instead of running in place, and zero-jitter camera-synchronized player mesh positioning with boundary-accurate keyframe interval interpolation.
- Generalized NPC stance & dual-channel animation blending (`NpcStanceResolver`, `SkeletonPoseEvaluator.EvaluateBlendedPose`, `EntityAnimationState`): convention-based auto-discovery across multi-stance mob families (Omega quadruped/biped, Uragnite shell, Hpemde open/submerged, Adamantoise guard), one-shot transition sequencing (`sp00`/`sp10`/`sp20`/`sp30`), strict priority interruption (`Death` > locomotion > stance transition), and zero-distortion local-space NLERP pose blending.
- **Resting and sitting ([#343](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/343)).** The entity's server status (0x00D / 0x00E `server_status`; the player's own from 0x037, copied onto its entity by `EntityPacketModule.HandleCharStatus` and each locomotion tick, since the server sends no 0x00D for the player) puts it in a seated category: 33 (LandSandBoat `xi.animation.HEALING`, set by the Healing effect that /heal adds) is `AnimationCategory.Healing`, 47 (`SIT`) is `Sitting` (`RestPosture.CategoryFor`). `AnimationStateClassifier` picks it after death, event poses and movement, before the battle stance. Each posture is three of the model's own Section 0x07 routines (`RestPosture`): Healing `res0` (sit down with `rx0`, 52 ticks / 0.87 s, then `rx1` with loop count 0), `res1` (`rx1` alone), `res2` (stand up with `rx2`, 56 ticks / 0.93 s, blend out 20 ticks); Sitting `sit0` (`si0`, 120 ticks, then `si1`), `sit1`, `sit2` (`si2`, 120 ticks). `EntityAnimationState` plays the enter routine's first clip as a one-shot transition when the category changes into the posture, then loops the loop routine's clip (`NpcStanceResolver.ResolveTargetClip`), and plays the exit routine's first clip when it changes out to a standing category; death cuts the transitions as before; movement would too, but the player is held in place while seated and while standing up (`EntityAnimationState.IsStandingUp`, `SeatedMovementHold`; [input](../input/console-and-input.md#moving-stands-you-up-from-heal-or-sit)), so moving out of /heal or /sit stands up first, then runs (the maintainer's retail check, round 1). An entity first seen already seated shows the loop without sitting down. A model without the clips stands idle (retail monsters 86-89 carry a `sit0` routine without its clip).
  - *Evidence* (the retail race skeletons, `RestPostureRealDataTests`, and a scan of model ids 1-3500, 2026-10-12): the same routine and clip names on all eight races (only the loop lengths differ, 1.33-2.00 s); fixed models carrying them include 24, 530, 761, 780, 1443-1445, 1596-1598, 1973, 1974, 1976, 1981, 1986, 1991 and 3275. The seated loops lower the head joint against the idle by 0.20 (Tarutaru `rx1`) to 0.69 yalms (Hume male `rx1`, 1.62 to 0.93). Which routine each status plays is **beyond xi-tools**, whose docs/anim/schedule.md describes the sit routine's shape only. Statuses from LandSandBoat `scripts/enum/animation.codegen.lua` and `c2s/0x0e8_camp.cpp` (the Healing effect's script sets the animation); a beastmaster's pet heals with its master (`pet_controller.cpp`) and shows the same posture if its model has the routines.
  - *Synthesis ([#315](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/315)):* status 44 (`xi.animation.SYNTH`, `RestPosture.StatusSynthesis`) takes the Healing posture: the retail video of a Tarutaru synthesizing (2026-10-16) sits in one short motion and, after the result, rolls back onto its back and stands, which is Tarutaru's `rx2`; no synthesis routine or clip exists in the DATs. S2C 0x030's `Status` is put on the crafter at once (`SynthesisAnimationController`). The raised hand with the crystal (`ri0?`) and the element effect come from the element's effect file 4912 + `EffectNum`, played as an action presentation that holds the body to its end ([session-and-packets.md](../network/session-and-packets.md#crafting-and-guild-shops-112)).

## Transient combat and action animation (Phase 5D.1)

S2C `0x028` actions play on their actor through the model's own **motion routines** (Section 0x07), then blend back to the stance; each result shows on its target when the actor's routine reaches its hit.

- **Motion routines (`MotionRoutineDecoder`, `EntityModel.MotionRoutines`).** Every model and motion pack carries named 0x07 routines: the swings `ati0`-`ati2` (standing) and `atf0` / `atb0` / `atl0` / `atr0` (moving), the counters `cni0` / `cnf0`..., the chants `cabk` / `cawh` / `cast`..., the releases `shbk` / `ssbk` / `shot`..., the item use `cait` / `shit`, the draw `out0` and sheathe `in 0`, the reactions `damg` / `gurd` / `pary` / `gur1`, death `dead`. The decoder reads op `0x05` (play clip: reference with a `?` body-region wildcard, `+0x10` f32 speed, `+0x18` u16 blend-in ticks, `+0x1C` blend-out, `+0x1E` loop count: n plays then holds the last frame, 0 loops until the next motion replaces it, xi-tools `docs/anim/schedule.md` `maxLoops`), the links `0x03` / `0x3B` / `0x57` / `0x3C` (followed into the same model; `0x3B` / `0x3C` wait for the child), a link to `dada` / `mdam` as the hit tick, op `0x21` / `0x25` flinch (`+0x18` f32 ticks) and op `0x5A` pose flash (`+0x0E` u16 pose, `+0x14` f32 ticks). Routines run at 60 Hz; delays are the wait after a command. A PC's routines come from its race base (`ROM/27/82` for Hume male) with the weapon's battle pack (`ROM/32/13`... per `CharacterEquipmentResolver`) overriding. Field layout read from the retail DATs (2026-09-28), with the op table from xi-tools `docs/fx/effect_system.md` and `docs/ability/mixer.md`.
  - Measured on Hume male: the swings play `at0?` / `at1?` / `at2?` and land at tick 34-40 of 88-118; each weapon pack maps its routines to its own clips (`ROM/32/15`'s `ati2` plays `at3?`), so clips are never hardcoded. The chants loop their clip 8-63 times (treated as sustained until replaced). Monster 1600: swings land at ticks 22-50; `cawh` / `cate` link `cast` (`ma0?` x10); `shwh` links `shot` (`ma1?` then `ma2?`); `gurd` / `pary` play the two-tick `gud?` pose with a 16-tick blend-out.
- **Dispatch (`ActionPlaybackQueue`, #10).** Subscribes to `CombatState.ActionExecuted` and posts `ActionRequest`s to the actor's `EntityAnimationState` (thread-safe queue, played on the render thread). By `cmd_no`: a basic attack posts one swing per result (a double attack or dual-wield round swings each hit in turn); the start packets (magic, item, ranged, readied skill) carry the actor's routine FourCC in `cmd_arg` (`cabk`, `cait`, `calg`, `cate`), or an `sp??` one when interrupted (the chant stops); a finished spell plays the release of the last chant (`ca??` to `sh??`, else `shot` / `shbk`); a finished item plays `shit`; weapon skills, monster TP moves, pet skills, job abilities and spells play their presentation DAT's `main` ([Action presentations](#action-presentations-132), #132), with the old stand-ins (a swing, the `cm0` clip, the chant's release) only where it plays nothing on the actor. Semantics from XiPackets `world/server/0x0028`.
  - Hits: the actor's state delivers a request's hits at its routine's hit tick (a routine without one, e.g. a release, at its second clip step or tick 36). When the actor is not animated the queue delivers them itself (`Update`, once per frame from the viewport): 0.7 s after arrival if no state took the request, 3 s if it waits in a queue, hit tick + 0.5 s once started. Requests that waited over 2 s are skipped with their hits shown; at most 3 wait behind the playing one.
- **Swing resolution (#11, #138).** The weapon type picks the battle pack (main weapon DAT Info byte 3, numbered per race: the same weapon look carries a different type on each race, and each race's pack table follows its own numbering), whose routines are the swings. Unarmed uses the race's hand-to-hand type (`CharacterEquipmentResolver.GetUnarmedWeaponType`: 2 Hume male, 3 Hume female, 0 Elvaan male, Tarutaru and Galka, 1 Elvaan female and Mithra; the type the hand-to-hand weapons, model 1 and 117-121, carry on that race). It used to be type 0 for every race, which on Hume male is the club and staff pack `ROM/32/13` (its draws `otd?` / `otb?` serve the two club and staff mounts), so an unarmed Hume fought with club swings and no kicks; kicks come unarmed too (XiPackets `0x0028` `sub_kind` 2 / 3). Standing: the result's `sub_kind` picks the family, the letter counting up from `a`: `ati*` main hand (0), `bti*` off hand (1), `cti*` right foot (2), `dti*` left foot (3); one of the family's routines at random (retail shows no fixed order). Only the hand-to-hand pack of each race carries `bti*` / `cti0` / `dti0` (Hume male `ROM/32/15`, Hume female `ROM/36/120`, Elvaan male `ROM/41/84`, Elvaan female `ROM/46/58`, Tarutaru `ROM/51/19`, Mithra `ROM/56/15`, Galka `ROM/60/112`; Elvaan male's alternate `ROM/98/117` and Tarutaru's `ROM/51/24` have `bti*` only); the dual-wield off-hand packs carry `bti*` too (see *Dual wield* below); every other pack and sub_kind (4, a thrown ranged weapon) falls back to `ati*`. Measured on Hume male `ROM/32/15` (2026-10-07, `HumeMaleHandToHand_OffHandAndKickSwings_MoveTheLeftHandAndTheFeet`, farthest travel from the battle stance in yalms): `ati0` (`at0?`) right hand 1.30, left 0.50; `bti0` (`at2?`) left hand 0.63, right 0.33; `bti1` (`at4?`) left 0.81, right 0.66; `cti0` (`wa4?`) right foot (joint 31) 2.27, left foot 0; `dti0` (`wa5?`) left foot (joint 37) 1.07, right foot 0. The basic-attack routine `atk0` itself links `hwat`, the sound `vatk`, op 0x24 (`IdleAtkSchSelect` in xi-tools `docs/reference/ps2_beta_2001.md`: the client selects the attack schedule) and op 0x32 (`IdleNextTarget`), so the choice is the client's, and the `a`-`d` letters are the evidence for it. Moving: the routine for the direction (`atf0` forward, `atb0` back, `atl0` / `atr0` strafe) whatever the sub_kind, which may keep playing while moving; with none, nothing plays and the hit still lands. Models without swing routines fall back to their `at0`-`at2` clips.
  - **Dual wield (#308).** A sub weapon held in the left hand (Info byte 3 set, mount not the shield mount 125) swaps the battle pack for a pair of dual-wield packs (`CharacterEquipmentResolver.TryGetDualWieldPackPaths`, `EntityModel.IsDualWielding`): the **main-hand pack** (engaged stance, `ati*`, moving swings `atf0`..., counters, damage poses) chosen by the main weapon, and the **off-hand pack** (`bti*`, moving `btf0` / `btb0` / `btl0` / `btr0`) chosen by the sub weapon. Hume male: main `ROM/108/112`-`115`, off hand `ROM/108/100`-`103`; the windows of every race are from xi-tools (`mv/lists/characters.json` "Dual Wield (Main)" / "(Sub)", its `dwMain` / `dwOff` motion categories), which gives no index rule. **Beyond xi-tools:** a weapon's pack is the rank of its animation type among the race's dual-wieldable types (Hume male 0 club, 1 sword, 3 dagger, 7 katana; Hume female 0, 1, 2, 6; Elvaan male 1, 2, 3, 9; Elvaan female 0, 5; Tarutaru 1, 3, 4; Mithra 0, 2, 3, 8; Galka 1, 2, 4). Checked on the retail data (2026-10-18, `DualWieldPackRealDataTests`): every race's sub weapons with a left-hand swing sound fall into exactly these types, and each pack's draw carries exactly its weapons' mounts (Info byte 6: draw clip `c` for the katana mounts 0x75 / 0x7C, `b` for the club mounts 0x72 / 0x7A, `a` for the sword and dagger mounts). **Differs from xi-tools:** its Mithra labels are swapped (`ROM/115/61`-`64` hold `bti*`). Main or sub types without a pack (an axe in the main hand on Hume male) keep the single-weapon pack.
    - *Swings.* The main-hand hit plays `ati*`, the off-hand hit (`sub_kind` 1) `bti*`; both packs' clips key the whole upper body, so every hit moves both arms, each weapon in its own hand (the clips hold the sub weapon's mount on the left hand, reference 126, through the stance and every swing: `HumeMaleTwoDaggers_UsesTheDualWieldPacks`). This is what the maintainer's DNC capture shows (`DNC_Dual_Wielding_Auto_Attacks.mp4`, 2026-10-16/17: both hits of a round use both hands, the left hand thrusting first; no mirrored swing). A pack need not start at variant 0 (the Hume male dagger packs carry `ati0` / `ati2` / `ati3` and `bti1`-`bti3`), so the family is found by any variant. The engaged stance is the main-hand pack's `btl` (both weapons low, as in the capture).
    - *Draw and sheathe.* The main-hand pack's own `in 0` / `out0` play no clip (72 ticks); each hand has a 36-tick routine, `in1N` / `ot1N` in the main-hand pack and `in2N` / `ot2N` in the off-hand pack, N the weapon's sub-type (Info byte 4; Tarutaru's type 1 pack has `in20`-`in22` for its three). The draw is the main hand's `i0?` then the off hand's `i1?`; the sheathe the off hand's `o0?` first, then the main hand's `o1?`. Checked by evaluating the retail clips (Hume male two daggers, 2026-10-18): `o0a` starts at the battle stance (summed joint gap 4.6, against 28.5 for `o1a`) and ends where `o1a` starts (1.9), `i0a` ends where `i1a` starts (1.9). `EntityModelLoader.AddDualWieldDrawRoutines` builds `in 0` / `out0` as the two in turn, the second linked at the first's length (72 ticks in all). Round 1 sheathed the main hand first from the wrong pose and, through blocking links counted twice, held the last pose for another 72 ticks: the sheathe "got stuck" for a couple of seconds (in-game test D5). They link the weapons' own sounds (`sinr` / `sotr`, `sinl` / `sotl`). Not captured in retail (the DNC capture starts engaged).
    - *Not loaded:* each pack's waist parts (the 4-8 files after the body packs, joints 38-47), as for the single-weapon packs.
- **One-shot playback and stance recovery (#12).** The action owns the body: each clip step blends in over its blend-in ticks, plays once and holds (or loops for a chant), and at the routine's end the body blends back to the stance clip over the last step's blend-out (the finished clip holds its last frame while fading: `PreviousClipLoops`). Death and a model change end it at once; moving ends a standing action; stopping ends a moving swing once its hit has landed. Engaging and disengaging while standing play the weapon draw `in 0` and sheathe `out0` ("in" into battle, "out" out of it: in every battle pack checked, `in 0`'s clip starts at the idle stance and ends at the battle stance and `out0`'s the reverse; Hume male unarmed, sword, scythe and great katana, Elvaan male and Galka sword, `DrawIsIn0_FromIdleToBattle_AndCarriesTheWeapon`, 2026-10-07). They used to be the other way round, so engaging played the sheathe: the weapon went from the hand to its mount and popped back at the end, a scythe sweeping through the body on the way ([#136](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/136)). The clips key the weapon joints themselves, carrying the grip from its mount to the hand (Hume male scythe: in the hand from about tick 9 of `inf?`), so no grip is re-parented while they play (`WeaponGripOverride` false); the battle stance and swings hold the grips at the hand anyway (grip joint on the hand, 0 degrees apart, in `btl` and `at0`), where the re-parenting changes nothing.
  - **Swing pace (#307).** Each melee swing plays its whole routine (guard, strike, back to guard), never cut, on a faster routine clock (`EntityAnimationState.ActionSpeed`; clips, hit tick, sounds, effects and weapon changes all follow it): a character's at twice the authored speed (`CharacterSwingSpeed`), a fixed model's at its authored speed, the hits of a round one after another. Faster only when the actor's rounds leave less time per hit than the swing takes (round interval / hits of the previous round, `SwingBudgetSeconds`; the hits of one 0x028 share an arrival time), at most 4x. Hand-to-hand plays one routine per hit, by its hand (`sub_kind` 0 `ati*`, 1 `bti*`), as retail's two-hit rounds show (an arms-up `ati2` then the crouching off-hand swing). Evidence: the maintainer's side-by-side captures of one fight in retail and GordianXI. At base speed (2026-10-20, Elvaan male MNK, Godhands) retail's two contacts land 0.70-0.73 s apart, where the routines put 86 ticks (1.43 s) between them: 1.96x; a two-hit round takes 1.8-1.95 s with no hold in guard between the hits. At full haste (2026-10-18) the same speed fits (two-hit rounds 1.6-2.0 s apart, hits 0.63-1.03 s apart): haste shortens the wait between rounds, not the strikes. Rounds 1 and 2 tuned a 0.9-1.0 s swing with round slots against GordianXI views that ran twice too fast: with one character popped out into a dedicated viewport the main window kept drawing the same world, and every viewport advanced the animations (`EntityAnimationState.ClaimFrameTime` now advances an entity once per frame in wall time). Values in [calibrations](../reference/calibrations.md#combat-animation).
  - Weapon skills, monster TP moves, pet skills, job abilities and spells play their own presentation DATs: see [Action presentations](#action-presentations-132) (#132).
- **Ranged attacks and weapon visibility (#158).** Weapons are shown and hidden by the actor's routines, not by a fixed rule. Op 0x75 (`HideWepControl`: u32 hide, u16 slot) works on weapon slots: 0 the main weapon (`wep0` meshes), 1 the sub (`wep1`), 2 the ranged weapon (`wep2`); a gear DAT's meshes are tagged with the slot they were equipped in (`AnimatedMeshGroup.WeaponSlot`). The routines mostly link the shared ones in `ROM/0/0.DAT` (`SharedWeaponRoutines`, checked against the retail file by `SharedWeaponRoutines_MatchTheRetailDat`): `hwpc` hides the ranged weapon, `hwmg` hides all three, `hwso` shows the ranged weapon and hides the main and sub, `hwat` / `stlg` / `splg` show main and sub and hide the ranged weapon.
  - *Default.* Every PC race base's `init` links `hwpc`, so the ranged weapon starts hidden (`EntityModel.DefaultHiddenWeaponSlots`, bit 2). It must be: the battle packs' stance clips (`btl1`...) hold its mount bone at scale 1 by the feet (Hume male joint 89 at about (-0.1, 0.06, 0) in `ROM/32/13`), and the race base stances hold it at scale 0.
  - *Ranged attack.* The start packet's `calg` runs `hwso` and op 0x76, the finish packet's `shlg` runs `hwso` and op 0x77; 0x76 / 0x77 run the race base's `lc<NN>` / `ls<NN>`, NN the ranged weapon's RangeType (xi-tools `docs/fx/effect_system.md` op list, `docs/ability/mixer.md`). RangeType is byte 14 of the ranged DAT's Info section (0x45; field from xi-model-viewer `ui/js/dat/inspect.js` `RANGE_TYPE`; values checked on the retail Hume male ranged DATs 2026-10-07): 1 flutes, 2 harps, 3 guns and crossbows, 4 / 5 throwing weapon / ammo, 6 bows, 8 one model (87, no `lc08` exists), 10 / 11 handbells, 0 the 3-vertex stubs (read as none: `lc00` / `ls00` are the singing routines). Hume male `ROM/27/82`: `lc06` plays `yu0?` once then holds `yu1?` (x8, sustained until the finish), `ls06` plays `yu2?` with the hit at tick 30 (its `ldad` link, the ranged `dada`); `lc03` / `ls03` play `gu0?`-`gu2?` (hit 34), `lc04` / `lc05` `na0?` / `na1?` with `ls04` `na2?` (hit 66) and `ls05` `na3?` (hit 66), `lc01` / `lc02` the instruments `sf?` / `sh?`, `lc11` / `ls11` the handbell `gc?` (own 0x75 ops), `lc10` the `gh?` geomancy motion with every weapon hidden.
  - *Which hand.* No re-parenting: the ranged clips themselves carry the mount bone (Hume male joint 89, Hume female 78, Elvaan male 72, Elvaan female 26, Tarutaru 45, Mithra 89, Galka 102; every ranged model with a mesh binds there, the bows also to its children for the string) onto a hand and scale it 0 to 1 and back. Measured by evaluating the retail clips (2026-10-07, `HumeMaleBow_IsHiddenUntilTheShot_AndTheShotPutsItInTheLeftHand`, `HumeMaleGun_GoesToTheRightHand`): in `yu0`-`yu2` the mount coincides with the left hand (joint reference 126) on Hume male, Galka and Tarutaru, at scale 1 (Galka 1.6, Tarutaru 0.7: the clips size the weapon per race) from about a third into `yu0` until the end of `yu2`; in `gu1` it coincides with the right hand (127). xi-tools `docs/gear/pose.md` states that nothing animates the mount and re-parents it onto the left hand for a drawn pose (**Differs from xi-tools**: the race base ranged clips `gu?` / `na?` / `yu?` / `sh?` / `sf?` / `gc?` do animate it; the survey it cites covered the base, movement and battle stances only).
  - *Playback.* `MotionRoutine.WeaponChanges` lists a routine's show / hide changes by tick (own op 0x75 and links to the shared routines); `EntityAnimationState.HiddenWeaponSlots` applies them as the routine plays and returns to the model's default when the action ends, and `EntityRenderer` skips the hidden slots' meshes. So a cast or item use also hides the main and sub weapons (`cabk` / `cait`... link `hwmg`), and a ranged attack hides them while the ranged weapon is out. The melee weapons come back with the next swing (every battle pack's `atk0` and `cnt0` link `hwat`); GordianXI also restores the default when a routine ends, since the stance in between has no routine of its own to do it. The sustained `lc<NN>` is replaced by `ls<NN>` when the finish packet arrives.
- **Hit reactions (#13).** A hit with distortion (`scale & 3`: 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1) flinches the target toward the damage pose `dfm` (attacker in front) or `dbm` (behind) over the model's `damg` flinch length (24 ticks), rising over the first quarter and easing back. The flinch is additive: each joint turns by the pose's offset from its neutral twin (`dfi` / `dbi`, `dfi6` / `dbi6` on the PC motion packs) on top of whatever is playing. Measured: `dfm` and `dfi` differ on 4-11 joints of the monsters checked while both sit 25-35 degrees off the stance clips, so an absolute blend would drag the whole body into another stance. Guard / parry / block play their routine as a pose flash (PC op `0x5A`: pose 0 `gdm`, 1 `pym`, 16 ticks; monsters their `gud?` clip, absolute); poses without a twin blend absolutely, masked to the joints they really drive (the loader grafts idle tracks into PC battle-pack poses). A counter reaction makes the target play its counter swing. Misses and spikes show nothing on the body. Checked by CPU-skinning Hume male and monster 1600 poses offscreen (2026-09-29); in-game check pending.
- **Engagement end (#67).** The local player's engagement ends (no disengage request is sent; the server has already ended it) and the fought target is dropped when S2C `0x037` status leaves 1 (engaged), or the engaged target dies (`Hpp` 0 or status 2 / 3) or despawns; the stance change then plays the sheathe. A selected target that despawns is dropped too. Status values from LandSandBoat `data/enums/animation.yaml`.
- **Event poses (#86).** A running event (cutscene) can own an entity's place: `WorldEntity.EventPose` (position, heading, walk speed) replaces the server position and heading while set, drawn on the floor below it (`EntityGrounding.GetDisplayHeight(Vector3, ...)`); the walk speed picks the walk (up to 2.5 yalms/s) or run clip, else idle. `IsEventHidden` keeps the entity from being drawn. The local player's controller position is left alone (it is what the client reports). Both are set and cleared by `EventDialogController`; see [events/vm.md](../events/vm.md#cutscene-staging).
- **Body-region clip parts (#163).** Fixed NPC models (a monster-style model id rather than a race and gear look: Curilla model 69, Prince Trion model 64, 179 of the 1,222 models with clips in ids 1-1499) store each motion as body-region parts, e.g. `wlk0` (16 joints: legs), `wlk1` (73: upper body, arms and the weapon joints), `wlk2` (10: waist). The stem name (`wlk`) used to point at the first part in file order, so a walk moved the legs alone with the upper body in its bind pose and the sword at the floor, `run` on Curilla was the waist part, and a model whose first part is the waist slid without a walk cycle. `EntityModelLoader.MergeBodyRegionParts` now joins the parts of a stem into one clip when they animate disjoint joints (every joined stem in the survey is such a set: `idl`, `wlk`, `run`, `btl`, `at00`-`at02` and so on); each part name counts once, the last copy in file order, since some models store every part twice (the Tarutaru of Lower Jeuno event 70, models 1443-1445, carry two of each `idl0`-`idl2`, `wlk0`-`wlk2`, `run0`-`run2`; the copies overlapping each other kept them from joining, so Makki-Chebukki's `run` was the waist part alone and her legs never ran, in-game test 2026-10-03, [#197](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/197)); same-stem clips that move the same joints stay separate motions, and then the stem plays part 0 (`EntityModelLoader.AddClipsWithStems`; it was the first part in file order, so the elemental model 11, `ROM/97/61`, which stores two whole-body idles `idl1` before `idl0`, idled on `idl1`; a stem never replaces a clip of that exact name). The join runs for every model loaded by id, so it also covers the Trusts and notorious monsters of [#75](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/75) (checked on the retail DATs 2026-10-03, `SplitIdle_DrivesEveryPart`, and by CPU-skinning Shantotto's idle: arms down at her sides instead of the bind pose's spread arms): Shantotto (model 3000, `ROM/309/20`: `idl0` 14 joints, `idl1` 69, `idl2` 10) and Naji (3001) idle with all 93 / 94 joints, Alexander (1834: `idl0` 74, `idl1` 7) and the Doppelganger model 547 with every part. A scan of the retail model DATs (2026-09-28) found 308 with disjoint `idl0` / `idl1` and 22 with overlapping ones. `NpcStanceResolver` asks for the stem before part 0 everywhere, death included (`ded` before `ded0`, `de1` before `de10`). Player looks were not affected: their packs are merged by `MergeLocomotionCategories`. Event motion banks store their gestures the same way (Curilla's bank 140, file 32244: `tlk0` legs, `tlk1` upper body with the sword joints 84 / 96, `tlk2` waist), and `EventMotionBank` joins them with the same rule (`EntityModelLoader.JoinBodyRegionParts`); with the legs part alone her arms and sword fell to the bind pose while she talked.

- **Event gestures (#165).** An event's 0x2C / 0x5B / 0x66 post an `ActionMotion.EventMotion` request: it ends the action playing at once and plays the named routine from the event motion banks loaded onto the entity (`EntityAnimationState.AddEventMotionBank`, `EventMotionBank`: a `mot_` DAT's 0x07 routines and their 0x2B clips, named with the models' three-letter wildcard, e.g. `tl1?`), newest first, else the model's own; its clips come from that bank. `ActionMotion.EventMotionStop` (0x50) ends it by name. - **Event pose smoothing (#165).** The event VM moves a pose once per game tick (16-31 ms, irregular), which drawn as is made cutscene walks stutter and turns snap. `EventPoseSmoother` (render thread, per entity) follows a walking pose at its walk speed (up to 1.25 times, to catch up), eases turns toward the new heading (exponential, 8 per second: about 95% in 0.4 s; retail's turn rate is not measured) and takes a placement (a move while standing, or a jump farther than half a second of walking, at least 1.5 yalms) at once. The event's first pose starts from the heading the entity is drawn with when it leaves the entity within 1.5 yalms of where it stands (`EventPoseSmoother.Start`), so an NPC that 0x1E / 0x4A turns to face the player in the event's first tick eases round at the same 8 per second; a first pose farther away is a placement and takes its heading at once too. The server sends no turn for a talk (LandSandBoat turns an NPC only for `showText` / `lookAt` scripts, which ease through `WorldEntity.UpdateHeading`). When the event ends, an entity it placed snaps back to its server position and heading. An event walk (0x1F) moves its height in a line toward the goal's (the goal height is often not the floor's: the Southern San d'Oria knights walk from height -2 to a goal at 0 over a floor at -2 that steps down to 0 near the end; taking the goal height at once drew them 2 yalms under the floor, where no floor lies below), and an event pose is drawn on the floor within a normal step of it, else on one up to `EntityGrounding.EventStepUpHeight` (2.5 yalms) above it (`EntityGrounding.GetEventDisplayHeight`; searching only the wide range drew Port Jeuno 324's Buntz, who stands at his server spot at the foot of a ledge 1.2 yalms high, on top of the ledge, found in the maintainer's in-game test 2026-10-03).

- **Event head look (#174).** An event's look (`WorldEntity.EventLook`, opcodes 0x1E / 0x4A / 0x79) turns the entity's head toward the entity it looks at while its body keeps its heading. - **Event turn speed and fades ([#197](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/197)).** An event can set an entity's body turn speed (0x59 sub 0 / 1, `WorldEntity.EventTurnSpeed`). While it is set, `EventPoseSmoother` turns the body at that constant rate instead of the ease, read as 4096ths of a turn per 60 Hz frame (provisional, [calibrations](../reference/calibrations.md)). 0x6C fades an entity's colour alpha (`WorldEntity.EventAlpha`, 0x80 = opaque). `EntityRenderer` draws faded entities after all others: a depth-only pass, then the colour blended at alpha / 0x80 (the cutout shader's output alpha is `1 - SkyLayerParams.w`). - **Server flag bits that change the drawing ([#334](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/334), 2026-10-10; awaiting the in-game test).** From the S2C 0x00D / 0x00E flag words ([entity-flags.md](../reference/entity-flags.md)): **facetarget** (`WorldEntity.FaceTargetIndex`) turns the head toward that target index the way an event look does (`EntityRenderer.HeadTurn` / `FaceTargetOf`, same `HeadLook` limits and ease), only while no event look is set, the entity is not in the running event, not dead and not the local player; LandSandBoat sends an NPC's database `LookAt` and a player's current target. **MotStopFlag** (`IsMotionStopped`, LandSandBoat: Terror) freezes the pose: the animation is not advanced and the head look is held; hit reactions are still taken, without a flinch, so their sounds play (`TakeReactionsWhileStopped`, #328: the Macro Test dummies are kept in Terror). **Flags3 bit 31** (`IsHalfTransparent`, LandSandBoat `ghost_phase`) draws the entity at alpha 0.5 through the event fade path. **SleepFlag** and 0x00E **AutoPartyFlag** stop it being drawn (`IsDrawn`), as HideFlag does. **TurnFlag** is decoded but not used: every turn eases (`WorldEntity.UpdateHeading`). - **Knockback (#14).** Knockback is not a server placement: it is the upper 3 bits of each target result's `scale` field in S2C `0x028` (shared with hit distortion), and the client slides the character itself, then reports the new position via `0x015` (LandSandBoat trusts it). When a hit with a knockback level lands on the local player (`ActionPlaybackQueue.HitLanded`, wired in `CharacterSession`), `PlayerLocomotionController.ApplyKnockback` pushes it away from the attacker through `MoveHorizontally`, so walls (`ZoneCollisionMesh.ResolveWalls`), ledges and ground following apply as for walking; a server placement cancels the slide. Remote entities follow their server positions as usual.
  - Levels 1-7 (`KnockbackSettings`): push per tick / damper / timer from LSB `enums/action/knockback.h` (Level 1 = 0.083, 0.075, 5; Level 7 = 0.167, 0.05, 45; the same values as XiPackets' client table). How the client integrates them is undocumented; GordianXI moves the push each 60 Hz tick, shrinking it by the damper fraction per tick, for the timer's ticks: about 0.36 yalm at level 1, 1.05 at level 4, 3.0 at level 7. Calibration against a retail capture pending.
  - **Anchor toggle (built-in Windower "Anchor"):** `/anchor [on|off]` ignores knockback; off by default for legacy parity.
  - **Server kill-switch:** `FeatureRestrictions.KnockbackOverride` (bit 8) keeps knockback on whatever `/anchor` says, following the collision-toggle pattern (`CollisionSettings`).
  - Out of scope by design: discarding WPOS (`0x05B`/`0x065`) placements. Ignoring them would defeat monster draw-in, GM/script teleports and event positions, so GordianXI always honors them and offers no toggle.

### Action presentations (#132)

A finish packet's result carries the presentation to play in its 12-bit `sub_kind` (XiPackets `0x0028`: "the animation id to be played"). `ActionMotionLibrary` turns it into a DAT by category, reads it once (DAT bytes through `ZoneDatLoader.Load`, on the network thread when the action arrives) and caches it as an `ActionAsset`: the DAT's routines and clips (an `EventMotionBank`) and its routines read as effect timelines (`EventSceneResource`). Status: built and checked against the retail DATs; **not yet seen in game** (the menus that trigger these actions are other lanes' work; a GM `!injectaction` or a monster's TP move can test it).

- **Motion.** `EventMotionBank.BuildOnModel` flattens the DAT's `main` for the actor with links reaching the DAT's routines first, then the actor's own, and clips taken from the DAT first, then the actor's: a weapon skill plays its own clips (Fast Blade on Hume male `ROM/76/30`: `b00?` at 1, `b02?` at 35, the parts 0-1 of the body file joined with part 2 from companion `ROM/76/124`, hit through `mdam` at 80); a job ability plays the actor's `cm0?` / `cm1?` (Berserk `ROM/15/89`); a spell links the caster's release with op 0x3C (Cure `ROM/10/9` runs Hume male's `shwh`: `mw1?`, `mw2?`; Fire `ROM/11/17` its `shbk`: `mb1?`, `mb2?`), so the release now follows the spell, not the last chant; a monster move plays the family's clips from the DAT (the crab's Bubble Shower `ROM/14/50`: `wz10`, `wz20`, `wz30`, keyed on its 48 joints) or the actor's own (Big Scissors `ROM/14/52`: the crab's `ma0`, `ma2`, `at2`, hit at 154). The built routine is cached per model. When it plays no clip on the actor (a monster without the release a spell links; a job ability on a model without `cm0`), the stand-in plays. A DAT clip that keys a joint the actor's skeleton lacks is not played (a move file of another family). Checked by evaluating the clips on the retail skeletons (2026-10-11): the farthest joint travel from the battle stance stays in the range of the race's own swing `at0` (Fast Blade's `b02` 1.64 yalms on Hume male vs 1.32 for `at0`; Galka 46: 2.68 vs 2.60; Tarutaru 259: 0.74 vs 0.96; crab 186: 0.52 vs 1.31), so no clip is authored for another skeleton.
- **Body and result.** A presentation gives the body back at the end of its last clip step (`EntityAnimationState.BodyEndTick`), not at its routine's length, which runs on for the effects (Cure's `main` is 245 ticks, its release ends at 121). Its result shows at the routine's own hit tick: when the body is free earlier, the request's hits are handed to the queue (`ActionRequest.DetachHits`), which shows them exactly at the hit tick (Cure's heal at 205, about 1.4 s after the caster lowers its hands). - **Effects.** The renderer (`ZoneTerrainRenderer.ActionEffects.cs`) starts an `ActionEffectPlayer` when a drawn entity begins an action whose presentation has effect routines (`ActionEffectTracker`, once per `ActionSerial`): the DAT's generators (`ResourceManager.GetSceneEffects`, decoded ahead on the network thread through `ActionMotionLibrary.EffectsPreloader`) run `main` with the command walk of the cutscene scene files (`SceneEffectPlayer`: 0x02 start a generator, 0x03 / 0x73 start a routine, 0x1E / 0x3F / 0x5F kill). Source-side generators ride the actor at their source joint reference; target-side ones (attach types 2, 4, 6) ride the action's first target at their target joint reference, each simulated in its own actor's model space.
