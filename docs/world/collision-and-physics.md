# Collision & Ground Physics

> Collision soup decode, ground following, falling, walls, elevators, camera collision and server position authority (Phase 5F). Status and open work: [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) and GitHub Issues.

## World collision and navigation (Phase 5F)

- **Collision Mesh Decode:** `ZoneCollisionDecoder` reads the retail player-collision soup from the ZoneDef collision block (header `+0x08`: local-space meshes placed by `0xC0` transforms through (transform, mesh) pair groups; per-triangle wall bit, terrain type and camera transparency), layout referenced from xi-tools `docs/zone/collision.md`. This is the same soup LSB's navmesh and LoS are baked from, not the visible `0x2E` meshes. Verified against retail data: collision floors match the visible terrain with a median height difference of 0.000 yalms (Bibiki Bay 194k tris, Southern San d'Oria 106k tris).
- **Ground Height Sampling:** every moving locomotion tick settles the local player onto the highest walkable (up-facing) surface at most 0.5 yalms above its feet and at most 60 yalms below (faces up to 84° are ground, so steep banks and ledges are walked down; a riser that carries a 60° face stops the feet: *Short walls*, below), so it climbs stairs/slopes and drops off ledges without popping onto bridges or upper floors overhead. Level treads (stairs, curbs) round the feet over their edges with a 0.9-yalm foot sphere (`TryGetSteppedGround`), so stairs climb and descend as a ramp like the legacy client: calibrated on a Windower capture of Southern San d'Oria's stairs (mean error 0.034 yalms vs 0.217 for per-tread snapping); slopes keep exact heights. `WorldState.Collision` is handed over by the viewport once the zone loads and cleared on zone change; `PlayerLocomotionController.CollisionEnabled` turns it off. `ZoneCollisionService` gives every registered session (displayed or not) its own zone's collision through `ResourceManager.TryLoadZoneCollision`, which decrypts only the ZoneDef section and caches one mesh per zone. The orbital camera eases its follow height (rate 6/s, teleports > 8 yalms snap) so steps and slopes no longer jolt the view. `/moveto` without a height lands on the floor nearest the current height; a placement with a height (`/moveto x height y`, `!pos`) holds in mid-air until the player moves, then falls under gravity while horizontal movement continues (66 yalms/s² to a 30 yalms/s top speed, fitted to a Windower capture of 12- and 20-yalm falls; a fall continues after the keys are released), so a higher drop carries further forward, as with Project Tako's position hacks (lifting onto a ledge). Drops deeper than 0.5 yalms fall; stairs, slopes and curbs still settle at once. `!pos` and `/moveto` take the server's order (x, height, y), which LSB's `setPos` uses, and `!pos` is sent as typed ([#496](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/496); until then GordianXI took (x, y, height) and reordered it).
- **Terrain types:** each triangle's terrain (Object, Path, Grass, Sand, Snow, Stone, Metal, Wood, shallow and deep water, 10) picks the footstep sound and the foot effects (#40, #342): [audio.md](../design/audio.md#footsteps-40), [particles.md](../rendering/particles.md#footprints-and-foot-effects-40), table in [enums.md](../reference/enums.md#collision-terrain-types-collisiontriangleterrain). South Gustaberg's floors (zone 107, 2026-10-20) are Stone 141k triangles, Object 130k, Path 43k, Wood 16k, deep water 8k, Grass 7k.
- **Collision Toggles:** `/collision [ground|walls|entities|all] [on|off]` (`CollisionSettings`), per session. New server kill-switch bits `WallCollisionOverride` (keeps ground + walls on) and `EntityCollisionOverride` (keeps entity collision on) pin layers the server protects. Knockback slides go through the same wall and ledge checks; `/anchor` and the `KnockbackOverride` bit are described in [entities-and-animation.md](entities-and-animation.md#transient-combat-and-action-animation-phase-5d1).
- **Entity Collision (soft bump):** `EntityBumpCollision` stops the player on walking into a player, NPC, monster, pet or trust and lets it through after a hold of pushing; once passed through, another player never blocks again until the local player is more than 50 yalms from it (LandSandBoat's entity render distance) or leaves the zone, while NPCs and monsters block every time (the maintainer's retail observation, #16, clarified in the round 2 test, 2026-10-22). A passed-through player is remembered by server id, so a server despawn and respawn while near (LandSandBoat does this with entities every few seconds, Port Jeuno log, 2026-10-21) keeps it passable. Overlapped characters never block. Body radius by race for characters drawn from a race model (PCs, and NPCs that look like them): 0.3 Hume / Mithra, 0.4 Elvaan, 0.6 Galka, 0.2 Tarutaru (measured in retail, below; until #16's test rounds they were 0.35 Hume / Elvaan / Mithra, 0.65 Galka, 0.25 Tarutaru estimates), scaled by a player's small / medium / large build (`PlayerSizeScale`), the player's own included; by 0x00E `GraphSize` for fixed models (0.35 / 0.7 / 1.4). For a PC, `GraphSize` is only the build (LandSandBoat `look.size`), which is why race decides. The hold is two of the client's position updates, 0.5 s, the same for every model: a larger one stops the player further out but does not hold it longer (provisional: retail's ~0.7-0.8 s reads as two of its ~400 ms updates, and GordianXI sends C2S 0x015 every 250 ms; round 1 and 2 tests, 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22). Retail (Collision_Bump.mp4, Castoff Point, 2026-10-16): centre-to-centre stops of about 0.6-0.8 yalm against a Hume-size NPC, 1-1.5 against a Galka-size NPC and about 1 against a Galka PC (frame estimates; the Windower readout updates every ~0.3 s); holds of about 0.7 s small and up to about 2 s large, not separable from the player's input. Hidden, invisible and non-blocking (Flags3 bit 28, XiPackets) entities are skipped, and so are untargetable ones (Flags1 TargetOff, [#425](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/425)): Yahse Hunting Grounds' camp props (zone 260, the campfires, model 2411, and the fences, 2779, around (320, -200)) are nameless NPCs that LandSandBoat sends with `entityFlags` 0x81B (`untargetable` 0x800) and name byte 0x68 (Flags3 bits 27, 29, 30, not 28), and retail walks through them (round 2 test, 2026-10-22), while everything that stopped the player in Collision_Bump.mp4 (Nunana, Castoff Point, Pollinating Swarm, a PC) is targetable. *Inference*: the rule is read from those two sets. They are not part of the zone's collision soup: zone 260's ZoneDef collision objects map one to one onto its placements (object index = placement index), and none of them is at the camp. The hitbox byte (0x00E Flags2.g, 0x00D `ModelHitboxSize`, the client's `g * 0.1`) does not give the radii: LandSandBoat sends 0 for every NPC and 8 for every living monster, whatever its size, so it is not used here ([#16](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/16)). `/collision entities` turns it off (JA0Wait-style). **Retail with a distance readout (#16, round 1 and 2 tests, 2026-10-27).** The maintainer, playing a Tarutaru in retail with Windower's `distance` addon (`sqrt` of the client's squared target distance, shown to 0.1 yalm, rounded), ran straight into standing characters and stopped at 0.4 from a Tarutaru, 0.5 from a Hume, 0.5 from a Mithra and 0.8 from a Galka. Taken as a sum of the two radii with the Tarutaru player's radius equal to a Tarutaru NPC's, that gives Tarutaru 0.2, Hume and Mithra 0.3 and Galka 0.6. The same run in GordianXI, playing an Elvaan with the old radii, stopped at 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.7 / 1.0: exactly 0.35 + the old radius. Half of that 0.2 difference was the player's race (Elvaan 0.35 against Tarutaru 0.25 in the old model); the other 0.1 was the old radii being too large. Round 2, retail as an Elvaan against the same characters: 0.6 Tarutaru, 0.7 Hume, 0.7 Mithra, 1.0 Galka, each 0.4 more than the target's radius, so a stop is the sum of the two radii and an Elvaan is 0.4. GordianXI's old Elvaan readings were the same numbers (0.35 plus radii 0.05 too large). Zone 260's Waypoint (model 2420, a fixed model) read 0.7 in retail as a Tarutaru, 0.9 in retail as an Elvaan and 0.9 in GordianXI as an Elvaan. Both readouts are 3D, and the Waypoint stands at height 0.0 while the ground around it is about -0.3 to -0.5 (the fence NPCs beside it), so it gives no clean horizontal distance. With about 0.4 of height it fits the 0.35 small fixed-model radius in both clients, which is left unchanged.
- **Elevators & Moving Platforms:** ZoneDef placements whose BlockID (+0x34, xi-tools) starts with `@` are elevator parts, pulled out of the static scenery into `MovingPlatformGroups`. Each platform (FourCC, footprint, authored floor) travels between the two stops of the zone's Section 0x36 `@` record with the same id ([#66](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/66), see *Lift records* below), built by both the full and the collision-only loader; with no elevator entity it rests at the stop nearest its authored floor. A car without a usable record (every car in the zones below has one) falls back to the landing with the most floor area beside its shaft, at least 2 yalms from the authored floor (`MovingPlatforms.CreateFromLanding`; Metalworks 2.0 <-> -10.0, within 0.04 of the capture). LSB's elevator `0x00E` (look size 3) carries the platform FourCC, the leg's start (Earth seconds since the Vana'diel epoch) and travel time; animation 10 sends the car to its record's floor 0 and 11 to floor 1 (see *Animation and pairing* below). An elevator entity drives the car whose record has its FourCC; a car without a record is driven by the entity whose position its footprint holds (or is nearest to), with 10 = up. `MovingPlatforms` evaluates the live height at the captured retail speed (1.99 yalms/s, a 6-second climb inside LSB's 8-second leg), a leg seen arriving plays from its arrival (updates land 1.5-2.9 s after their whole-second stamp), a shaft is closed to walkers unless its platform is at their level (retail's shaft doors), the renderer draws the parts there, the local player rides the platform (idle too), ground queries treat it as a floor, and other characters standing on it are drawn riding it (the capture showed a stationary rider carried up and down). Doors (`_` BlockIDs, open/close routines) are still drawn static.
  - **Lift records (Section 0x36 `@`):** format referenced from xi-tools (`docs/zone/elevators.md`, `docs/zone/subareas.md` §1, after the PS2 client's `XiLiftActor::SetLift` / `KO_RectData`). The plaintext `RID` section: payload +0x10 = offset of the table; there a u32 count, 12 bytes of padding, then 0x40-byte entries: +0x00 position (3×f32), +0x10 turn about Y, +0x18 full box extents, +0x24 4-char id (`@` = lift), **+0x34 / +0x36 s16 floor 0 / floor 1**. Each stop is `s16 / 256 + position.Y`, an absolute height in raw zone coordinates (+Y down); the car's placement origin is moved to it (`ZoneInteraction.TryGetLiftFloors`, `MovingPlatforms.Create`). The box is a detection volume larger than the travel, not the range. The car's ZoneDef placement links to its record through its BlockID (+0x34). A record whose stops are equal (under 0.1 yalm apart), not finite, or missing leaves that car on the fallback; a short or malformed section yields no records.
  - **The lift records are not in the first 0x36 table.** A zone DAT has several 0x36 sections and the `@` records sit in their own: Metalworks' `e237` (under `even`, beside its shaft doors `_6ls`-`_6lv`), Palborough Mines' `l143` (under `lift`), Pso'Xja's `l009`, Davoi's `l149`, Fort Ghelsba's `z141`. The decoder reads every table for lifts (`ZoneInteractionDecoder.DecodeAllFromDat`); door blockers come from every table that holds no lift record (`ZoneInteractionDecoder.DecodeDoorTablesFromDat`; the lift tables also hold the shaft doors, which `EntersEmptyShaft` already covers). Until 2026-10-20 they came from the first table only, which left 101 zones (1,448 doors) with no door blocking at all, since their first table holds no door record: Alzadaal Undersea Ruins (zone 72) keeps all 33 in its second table `_072` (round 4 E2, walking through a closed door). A door box authored flat (height 0: Alzadaal's stone doors `_20t`-`_20w`, 1.73 x 0 x 1.5, whose halves are effect meshes) blocks as a doorway of `ZoneDoors.FlatDoorHeight` (4.225, the zone's other doors' height) standing on the box (*inference*). A body stopped at a door face is put `ZoneDoors.FaceTolerance` (0.002 yalms) outside it, and counts as standing in the doorway only that far inside ([#239](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/239), round 5 E2, 2026-10-20): it used to be put exactly on the face, which for a door turned 180 degrees (Alzadaal's staging-point gates `_20c` / `_20d`, Nyzul Isle's way in) came back a rounding error inside, and the next step let the player through as one a door had closed on.

- **Doors ([#15](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/15), 2026-10-03):** ZoneDef placements whose BlockID starts with `_` are door leaves, pulled out of the static scenery into `ZoneGeometry.DoorLeaves` (part = the leaf's place in ZoneDef order, except that a `...l` / `...r` pair puts the left leaf first). Each door has a Section 0x36 record (`ZoneInteractionDecoder`; magic `RID`, 0x40-byte entries: centre, Y turn at +0x10, full extents at +0x18, id at +0x24) and routines under `<zone code>/door/<id>/` (`DoorRoutineDecoder`: `open`, `clos`, `into`, `intc`; ops 0x0D rotate and 0x0C slide carry x, y, z and the part index), formats referenced from xi-tools `docs/zone/doors.md` and `subareas.md`. A door entity's 0x00E (SubKind 2) carries the door id at payload 0x30 (`TryGetDoorObjectId` → `WorldEntity.TransportId`).
  - *Drawing:* `DoorAnimator` starts each door in its `intc` pose, gives it its `into` pose at once when its entity is first seen open, plays `open` when the status turns to 8 and `clos` when it leaves 8, each leaf from where it is; moves are linear over their frames (easing unknown). The status is `WorldEntity.EventStatus` while an event set one (0x4C / 0x4D), else the server's animation (LandSandBoat: 8 open, 9 closed 7 s later). The renderer turns or slides each leaf about its placement origin in the placement's own frame, so mirrored leaves (scale -1) swing as their mirror image.
  - *Collision:* the collision soup has no door leaves (a ray through each of Metalworks' 19 doorways passes), so a closed door would not block. `ZoneDoors` makes each door's 0x36 box solid while its status is not 8, switching the moment the status changes, not with the leaves: in retail a player walks through a door as soon as it starts opening, and a closing door blocks before it is shut (maintainer, in-game, 2026-10-03). A door whose entity has not been sent does not block. It is part of the walls layer (`/collision walls off` turns it off). Verified offscreen on Metalworks `_6l0` and Southern San d'Oria `_6ea`; `ZoneDoorTests` checks the Metalworks data (19 doors, leaves leave the doorway's plane to one side).
  - *Targeting:* door entities are targets like NPCs (LSB names them, e.g. `Door:House`). They have no model, so the target cursor's tip is put at the door entity's own position, the middle of its doorway (`EntityRenderer.TargetAnchor`), as in a retail Metalworks screenshot (2026-10-03); before, a door could be targeted but showed no cursor, so cycling seemed to skip it.
  - *Data (2026-10-03, every retail zone):* 3,424 doors have `open` / `clos`, 3,031 also `into` / `intc` (317 with a non-zero closed pose); most two-leaf doors use one mesh twice, mirrored by a -1 scale, with equal targets for both parts. The routines' sounds (op 0x0B: a 0x3D section name, range at +0x14 / +0x18) play when the door's status turns to open or leaves it, at the door (`ZoneSoundTable.DoorRoutineSounds`, `GameAudioService.PlayDoorSounds`, round 5 E2). Not done: routine links (0x03) and generators (0x02), and the door routines other than these (`evst` / `eved` / `ints` / `inte` play only in place of a missing `open` / `clos` / `into` / `intc`, from a directory one level below the door: Lower Jeuno's street lamps, [lighting.md](../rendering/lighting.md#lower-jeunos-street-lamps-community-service)).
- **Zone lines ([#350](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/350), 2026-10-11):** the zone DAT's Section 0x36 records whose id starts with `z` are zone-line boxes (`ZoneInteraction.IsZoneLine`, `Contains`: the turned box test of the door volumes), read from every 0x36 table into `ZoneCollisionMesh.ZoneLineVolumes` (`ZoneLines.Select`, one per id) by both the full and the collision-only loader. Every locomotion tick `SessionNetworkManager.NotifyLocomotionChanged` hands the player's position to its `ZoneLineMonitor`; walking into a line sends C2S 0x05E once with the box's id and the position (`RequestZoneTransitionByZonelineAsync`), and the server answers with the zone change (S2C 0x00B, `PerformZoneTransitionAsync`). The screen fades to black from the request on, as retail does (`ZoneLoadingScreen` counts a pending request as loading; [#36 / the 2026-10-11 retail recording](../design/character-lobby.md#loading-screen-36)), while the character keeps moving. From the request on no C2S 0x015 position update is sent (`SessionNetworkManager.PositionSendsHeld`) until the zone change, or until a refusal ends the request: the maintainer's LandSandBoat map server log (2026-10-12) shows retail sending none after its request and GordianXI's rejected as "Character is disappearing" (LSB sets the character disappearing as it accepts the request). The client still sends a datagram every network tick meanwhile, holding only an empty keep-alive sub-packet (type 0, size 0, which LSB's parse loop stops at): the map server only sends in answer to the client's datagrams, so without them round 2 of in-game testing (2026-10-12) waited 10-16 s for the S2C 0x00B and showed the red link-dead mark (our 10 s silence check). Auto-run is cancelled at a zone change, and input does not move the player while the screen is black (`PlayerLocomotionController.InputBlocked`, set by the viewport). On arrival the player faces the S2C 0x00A heading (LSB's zone-line destination rotation, away from the line) and the camera is put behind it at once (`WorldState.ZoneEntered` → `PlayerLocomotionController.ArrivalFacingCount`; the view snaps its orbit smoothing), so held movement keys, which are camera-relative, walk into the new zone; until [#588](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/588) the camera kept the previous zone's angle and holding forward could run back into a wall (Port ↔ Lower Jeuno). The camera is put behind the heading again when the screen is revealed, and the view snaps the model's heading smoothing as well. LSB converts the zone line's arrival facing (radians in `data/zones/*/zone.yaml`, e.g. Port Jeuno → Lower Jeuno 3.653041) with `radianToRotation` (radians / 2π × 256 = wire 148), the convention GordianXI reads. **From the zone-in until the reveal no input moves or turns the player** (`PlayerLocomotionController.ArrivalHeld`, #588 round 3): `InputBlocked` starts only once the fade is fully black, and the maintainer's logs of 2026-11-21 showed the S2C 0x00A arriving before that (a cached zone: 0.36 s after the zone-line request, against the 1.0 s fade), so a key held through the line turned the player at the arrival point while the camera stayed behind the 0x00A heading (Valkurm Dunes → Selbina: 0x00A heading 63, turned to 1 within 0.13 s; on the next line it turned to 221). The hold ends at the reveal (`InputBlocked` true then false), after 3 s if no loading screen ever blacks the session (a headless client), and at most after 20 s (the loading screen's own limit). Round 1 of the 2026-11-20 test still saw the character facing other ways than the camera; the session path replayed headlessly (0x00B world clear, black-screen ticks, a real 0x00A through the parser: `ZoneArrivalFacingTests`) keeps entity, tick and camera on the 0x00A heading, so the client logs it for the next round: `Zone-in facing (zone-in|reveal)` and `Zone-in watch: heading a -> b` (LIFECYCLE, any change of the player's heading from the zone-in to 10 s after the reveal), and a `Zone-in view check` warning when the drawn heading or the camera is more than 15° off the tick's heading in the first 300 frames.
  - *Lines and landings.* The records come in pairs numbered by the id's last character in base 36: the even one is the line, the odd one after it a small box beyond the next zone's line where the player arrives. LandSandBoat's zone lines (`data/zones/*/zone.yaml` `zonelines`, keyed by the same four characters as little-endian bytes) have `from` = the even box's centre exactly and `at` = an odd box's centre (East Ronfaure `z2t2` lands at Southern San d'Oria's `z6e1`, 110.234 / -2.127 / -54.223). A census of zones 1-299 (2026-10-11): 1685 `z` records, 845 even and 840 odd; all 844 LSB lines are even DAT ids, no odd id is an LSB line, and the one even id LSB lacks is `z7f2` in Kamihr Drifts (267), the zone LSB's handler notes for an invalid line. So only even ids are sent (`ZoneLines.IsCrossing`); an odd one would be refused. **Beyond xi-tools:** `docs/zone/subareas.md` names the `z` kind but not the pairing.
  - *Once per crossing.* The monitor arms only after the player has been seen outside every line of the zone (arriving on a line never sends the player back), fires once on entering a line, then keeps quiet while the request is pending (until the zone changes, the server places the player, or 15 s pass, a safety limit) and until the player has left the line. Nothing is sent while the session is not `ActiveInWorld`, a zone change is under way, movement is locked (an event) or an event zone is open; LandSandBoat blocks the request in an event.
  - *Refusals.* LandSandBoat (`packets/c2s/0x05e_maprect.cpp`) refuses an unknown id, a position more than 40 yalms from the line's origin, a closed or full destination, Monstrosity, or a rental chocobo into a no-mount zone: it prints "could not enter" (S2C 0x053) and sends the player's position back turned 180° (S2C 0x065 reset), so the player stands in the line facing out. That placement (`LocalPlayerState.PositionCorrectionCount` changing while the request waits) ends the pending request, so the screen fades back in; the monitor does not resend until they walk out and back in.
  - *Arrival.* The S2C 0x00A position is the centre of the landing box (e.g. `z6e1`, 4 yalms tall: about two yalms above the street), so the player is settled onto the floor under it once the new zone's collision is loaded (`LocalPlayerState.RequestGroundSettle`, set as the login's zone arrives; `PlayerLocomotionController.SettleOnGround`, no fall). Round 1 of in-game testing (2026-10-12) saw the player floating there, as a zone-in position held in mid-air like a GM placement; retail stands on the ground. Later placements still hold in mid-air until the player moves. The settle also snaps the view's camera follow height and position smoothing (`ArrivalSettleCount`), and the loading screen fades in only once it has run (`ArrivalReady`; a zone without collision has nothing to settle), so the arrival shows no drop or camera jump (round 2). - **Server Position Authority:** LandSandBoat trusts the client's reported position (`0x015` is stored as sent, no server-side collision), so its only corrections are WPOS (`0x05B`/`0x065`) placements: GM/script `setPos`, monster draw-in, event-end positions. WPOS is broadcast to everyone in range, so it is now routed by `UniqueNo` (previously every WPOS moved the local player, e.g. when another player was drawn in); other entities `Warp`. Local placements queue on `LocalPlayerState` and apply at the start of the next locomotion tick, so an in-flight movement tick cannot overwrite them or report a stale position (LSB ignores `0x015` only until the WPOS is sent). Modes per XiPackets: `0x0A` rotates only, `0x08` locks movement until `0x09`/`0x05` (or a zone change), and a charmed player ignores input and takes its own `0x00D` positions.
- **Remote Entity Grounding:** Other players, NPCs and monsters are drawn on the collision floor under their reported position (`EntityGrounding`, same step-up and stair rounding as the local player), not at the reported height: a Windower capture shows a player position-hacked 10 yalms up drawn walking on the floor by another client, before and after a teleport. Actors with the server's GroundFlag (Flags0 bit 15, "ignores world collision", XiPackets) and doors/elevators/ships keep the reported height; display only, server positions untouched.
- **Collision Acceleration Structure:** `ZoneCollisionMesh` indexes the soup in a 4-yalm uniform XZ grid (CSR arrays, allocation-free queries), so a query scans only the triangles in the query cell.
- **Camera Collision:** the orbital camera raycasts from its look-at point to its orbit position against the collision soup (`ZoneCollisionMesh.TryRaycast`, grid cells under the segment, double-sided, skipping camera-transparent triangles) and pulls in 0.3 yalms in front of the first hit; it snaps in and eases back out at 10 yalms/s, so it no longer swings outside cave walls.
- Dedicated `World/Collision` test coverage (synthetic decode, ground queries, locomotion grounding, retail-data alignment). Most of it drives `PlayerLocomotionController` or the camera, so it is in `Gordian.Engine.Tests`; remote entity grounding and zone lines are in `Gordian.Core.Tests`.
