# Collision & Ground Physics

> Collision soup decode, ground following, falling, walls, elevators, camera collision and server position authority (Phase 5F). Status and open work: [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) and GitHub Issues.

## World collision and navigation (Phase 5F)

- **Collision Mesh Decode:** `ZoneCollisionDecoder` reads the retail player-collision soup from the ZoneDef collision block (header `+0x08`: local-space meshes placed by `0xC0` transforms through (transform, mesh) pair groups; per-triangle wall bit, terrain type and camera transparency), layout referenced from xi-tools `docs/zone/collision.md`. This is the same soup LSB's navmesh and LoS are baked from, not the visible `0x2E` meshes. Verified against retail data: collision floors match the visible terrain with a median height difference of 0.000 yalms (Bibiki Bay 194k tris, Southern San d'Oria 106k tris).
- **Ground Height Sampling:** every moving locomotion tick settles the local player onto the highest walkable (up-facing) surface at most 0.5 yalms above its feet (a little more forgiving than retail, which blocks the low side walls of San d'Oria's ramps) and at most 60 yalms below, so it climbs stairs/slopes and drops off ledges without popping onto bridges or upper floors overhead. Level treads (stairs, curbs) round the feet over their edges with a 0.9-yalm foot sphere (`TryGetSteppedGround`), so stairs climb and descend as a ramp like the legacy client: calibrated on a Windower capture of Southern San d'Oria's stairs (mean error 0.034 yalms vs 0.217 for per-tread snapping); slopes keep exact heights. `WorldState.Collision` is handed over by the viewport once the zone loads and cleared on zone change; `PlayerLocomotionController.CollisionEnabled` turns it off. `ZoneCollisionService` gives every registered session (displayed or not) its own zone's collision through `ResourceManager.TryLoadZoneCollision`, which decrypts only the ZoneDef section and caches one mesh per zone. The orbital camera eases its follow height (rate 6/s, teleports > 8 yalms snap) so steps and slopes no longer jolt the view. `/moveto` without a height lands on the floor nearest the current height; a placement with a height (`/moveto x y z`, `!pos`) holds in mid-air until the player moves, then falls under gravity while horizontal movement continues (66 yalms/s² to a 30 yalms/s top speed, fitted to a Windower capture of 12- and 20-yalm falls; a fall continues after the keys are released), so a higher drop carries further forward, as with Project Tako's position hacks (lifting onto a ledge). Drops deeper than 0.5 yalms fall; stairs, slopes and curbs still settle at once. `!pos` takes Windower order (x, y, z = height; commas and parentheses accepted, as with `/moveto`) and is reordered for LSB's `setPos` (x, height, y).
- **Collision Toggles:** `/collision [ground|walls|entities|all] [on|off]` (`CollisionSettings`), per session. New server kill-switch bits `WallCollisionOverride` (keeps ground + walls on) and `EntityCollisionOverride` (keeps entity collision on) pin layers the server protects. Knockback slides go through the same wall and ledge checks; `/anchor` and the `KnockbackOverride` bit are described in [entities-and-animation.md](entities-and-animation.md#transient-combat-and-action-animation-phase-5d1).
- **Entity Collision (soft bump):** `EntityBumpCollision` stops the player on walking into a player, NPC, monster, pet or trust, lets it through after 0.35 s of pushing, then grants 1.5 s of free movement for crowds; overlapped characters never block. Size from 0x00D/0x00E `GraphSize`; hidden, invisible and non-blocking (Flags3 bit 28, XiPackets) entities are skipped. Radii (0.35/0.7/1.4 by size) and timings are estimates pending a retail capture. `/collision entities` turns it off (JA0Wait-style).
- **Elevators & Moving Platforms:** ZoneDef placements whose BlockID (+0x34, xi-tools) starts with `@` are elevator parts, pulled out of the static scenery into `MovingPlatformGroups`. Each platform (FourCC, footprint, authored floor) travels between the two stops of the zone's Section 0x36 `@` record with the same id ([#66](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/66), see *Lift records* below), built by both the full and the collision-only loader; with no elevator entity it rests at the stop nearest its authored floor. A car without a usable record (every car in the zones below has one) falls back to the landing with the most floor area beside its shaft, at least 2 yalms from the authored floor (`MovingPlatforms.CreateFromLanding`; Metalworks 2.0 <-> -10.0, within 0.04 of the capture). LSB's elevator `0x00E` (look size 3) carries the platform FourCC, the leg's start (Earth seconds since the Vana'diel epoch) and travel time; animation 10 sends the car to its record's floor 0 and 11 to floor 1 (see *Animation and pairing* below). An elevator entity drives the car whose record has its FourCC; a car without a record is driven by the entity whose position its footprint holds (or is nearest to), with 10 = up. `MovingPlatforms` evaluates the live height at the captured retail speed (1.99 yalms/s, a 6-second climb inside LSB's 8-second leg), a leg seen arriving plays from its arrival (updates land 1.5-2.9 s after their whole-second stamp), a shaft is closed to walkers unless its platform is at their level (retail's shaft doors), the renderer draws the parts there, the local player rides the platform (idle too), ground queries treat it as a floor, and other characters standing on it are drawn riding it (the capture showed a stationary rider carried up and down). Doors (`_` BlockIDs, open/close routines) are still drawn static.
  - **Lift records (Section 0x36 `@`):** format referenced from xi-tools (`docs/zone/elevators.md`, `docs/zone/subareas.md` §1, after the PS2 client's `XiLiftActor::SetLift` / `KO_RectData`). The plaintext `RID` section: payload +0x10 = offset of the table; there a u32 count, 12 bytes of padding, then 0x40-byte entries: +0x00 position (3×f32), +0x10 turn about Y, +0x18 full box extents, +0x24 4-char id (`@` = lift), **+0x34 / +0x36 s16 floor 0 / floor 1**. Each stop is `s16 / 256 + position.Y`, an absolute height in raw zone coordinates (+Y down); the car's placement origin is moved to it (`ZoneInteraction.TryGetLiftFloors`, `MovingPlatforms.Create`). The box is a detection volume larger than the travel, not the range. The car's ZoneDef placement links to its record through its BlockID (+0x34). A record whose stops are equal (under 0.1 yalm apart), not finite, or missing leaves that car on the fallback; a short or malformed section yields no records.
  - **The lift records are not in the first 0x36 table.** A zone DAT has several 0x36 sections and the `@` records sit in their own: Metalworks' `e237` (under `even`, beside its shaft doors `_6ls`-`_6lv`), Palborough Mines' `l143` (under `lift`), Pso'Xja's `l009`, Davoi's `l149`, Fort Ghelsba's `z141`. The decoder reads every table for lifts (`ZoneInteractionDecoder.DecodeAllFromDat`); door blockers still come from the first table only (the later tables also hold the shaft doors, which `EntersEmptyShaft` already covers).
  - **Evidence:** Metalworks (237) `@6l0` / `@6l1`: 3856 / 798 on Y -13.10 gives **+1.9625 / -9.983**; the Windower capture had riders between **1.962 and -9.983**, an exact match (the heuristic gave 2.0 / -10.0).     **Differs from xi-tools** (which has each car parked on one of its stops): Davoi's car is authored at -8.19, a yalm below its upper stop; the stops are absolute, not offsets from the authored car (relative stops would put it at -8.19 / -0.99, off the landings). With no elevator entity it now rests at -9.17. The stops are tested from both loaders in `MovingPlatformTests.ZoneLifts_TakeTheirStopsFromTheRecord` (skipped without the retail install).
  - **Animation and pairing (2026-10-04, after the first in-game test):** with 10 = up, the lever lifts started opposite to a retail client run side by side. *Evidence:* LSB's elevator handler (`transports/elevator_handler.cpp`) sends 10 while a lift ascends and 11 while it descends, except for lifts flagged `reversed` (Davoi, Palborough Mines and Fort Ghelsba, `data/zones/*/npcs.yaml`), where 10 means descending and 11 ascending; at start-up it reads the data's animation as the floor the car is on (11 → bottom, then flipped for `reversed`). In every record floor 0 (+0x34) is the bottom stop. The test logs show LSB spawning `@3z0` and `@450` with animation 11 and stamp 0. *Inference:* the client sends the car to floor 0 on 10 and floor 1 on 11, and pairs the entity with the car by FourCC. That fits the lever lifts (11 = top, as LSB means for `reversed`) and, with FourCC pairing, keeps Metalworks exactly as before: LSB places entity `@6l0` in the north shaft (its car `@6l1`), the two shafts always run opposite legs, and car `@6l0` going to floor 0 on 10 while car `@6l1` goes to floor 1 on the other entity's 11 is the same picture as the north car going up on 10 (`MovingPlatformTests.Metalworks_PairedByFourCc_PlaysAsBefore`). Before any entity arrives a car rests at the stop nearest its authored height (Davoi top, Palborough Mines bottom, Fort Ghelsba top); LSB keeps elevators always relevant, so this lasts only until the entity's first update. What retail shows in that gap is unknown.
  - **Events on a lift:** an event places its actors (the player included, e.g. turning to a lever) on the floor below their event position (`EntityGrounding.GetEventDisplayHeight`). That query ignored moving platforms, so a rider answering Palborough Mines' lever question was drawn on the pit far below the car until the event ended (the controller's own position was right throughout). The bug was older than #66. The event query now takes the platforms with the same rule as other characters: a car under the event position, within a step, at or above the static floor, wins; an actor riding a car (the local player's `RidingPlatformId`, a remote's) stays on it while over its footprint.
  - **Open:** the lever lifts (Palborough Mines, Davoi, Fort Ghelsba) need another in-game check after the animation fix. Move time: each shaft's `mv01` / `mv10` routines (`wt_b/even/@6lN/`) run op 0x1D (LiftMoveDriveTask) for 480 frames (8 s at 60 Hz) and LSB's leg is also 8 s, but the capture measured a steady 1.99 yalms/s (a 6.0 s climb); the captured speed stays until a capture explains the difference. **Differs from xi-tools:** it calls lifts client-side with no server packet or player trigger; under LSB every lift is a server elevator entity and Davoi / Palborough Mines / Fort Ghelsba's are lever-operated (`RunElevator`), so playback stays entity-driven.
- **Doors ([#15](https://github.com/jimmy58663/GordianXI/issues/15), 2026-10-03):** ZoneDef placements whose BlockID starts with `_` are door leaves, pulled out of the static scenery into `ZoneGeometry.DoorLeaves` (part = the leaf's place in ZoneDef order, except that a `...l` / `...r` pair puts the left leaf first). Each door has a Section 0x36 record (`ZoneInteractionDecoder`; magic `RID`, 0x40-byte entries: centre, Y turn at +0x10, full extents at +0x18, id at +0x24) and routines under `<zone code>/door/<id>/` (`DoorRoutineDecoder`: `open`, `clos`, `into`, `intc`; ops 0x0D rotate and 0x0C slide carry x, y, z and the part index), formats referenced from xi-tools `docs/zone/doors.md` and `subareas.md`. A door entity's 0x00E (SubKind 2) carries the door id at payload 0x30 (`TryGetDoorObjectId` → `WorldEntity.TransportId`).
  - *Drawing:* `DoorAnimator` starts each door in its `intc` pose, gives it its `into` pose at once when its entity is first seen open, plays `open` when the status turns to 8 and `clos` when it leaves 8, each leaf from where it is; moves are linear over their frames (easing unknown). The status is `WorldEntity.EventStatus` while an event set one (0x4C / 0x4D), else the server's animation (LandSandBoat: 8 open, 9 closed 7 s later). The renderer turns or slides each leaf about its placement origin in the placement's own frame, so mirrored leaves (scale -1) swing as their mirror image.
  - *Collision:* the collision soup has no door leaves (a ray through each of Metalworks' 19 doorways passes), so a closed door would not block. `ZoneDoors` makes each door's 0x36 box solid while its status is not 8, switching the moment the status changes, not with the leaves: in retail a player walks through a door as soon as it starts opening, and a closing door blocks before it is shut (maintainer, in-game, 2026-10-03). A door whose entity has not been sent does not block. It is part of the walls layer (`/collision walls off` turns it off). Verified offscreen on Metalworks `_6l0` and Southern San d'Oria `_6ea`; `ZoneDoorTests` checks the Metalworks data (19 doors, leaves leave the doorway's plane to one side).
  - *Targeting:* door entities are targets like NPCs (LSB names them, e.g. `Door:House`). They have no model, so the target cursor's tip is put at the door entity's own position, the middle of its doorway (`EntityRenderer.TargetAnchor`), as in a retail Metalworks screenshot (2026-10-03); before, a door could be targeted but showed no cursor, so cycling seemed to skip it.
  - *Data (2026-10-03, every retail zone):* 3,424 doors have `open` / `clos`, 3,031 also `into` / `intc` (317 with a non-zero closed pose); most two-leaf doors use one mesh twice, mirrored by a -1 scale, with equal targets for both parts. Not done: the routines' sounds (0x0B, no audio yet), routine links (0x03) and generators (0x02), and the door routines other than the four (`evst` / `eved`...).
- **Horizontal Wall/Obstacle Collision:** `ZoneCollisionMesh.ResolveWalls` pushes a column of 0.53-yalm body spheres (from the 0.5 step height to 1.6 yalms; the radius measured from a Windower capture of a character pressed into a Southern San d'Oria corner, 0.529/0.535 yalms from the walls) horizontally out of wall-like triangles (|normal.Y| < 0.5) in sub-steps of half a radius, sliding along walls; one-sided like the client (only front faces block). Anything below the step height is left to ground following, a move onto no floor at all is refused, and a sub-step whose accumulated push would cross a wall's front face is undone (a body wedged against a prop could otherwise be shoved through a thin wall). Verified on retail data: 0 of 600 walls in Southern San d'Oria and Bibiki Bay passed through, the retail stair climb is unobstructed, ~14 µs per move.
- **Server Position Authority:** LandSandBoat trusts the client's reported position (`0x015` is stored as sent, no server-side collision), so its only corrections are WPOS (`0x05B`/`0x065`) placements: GM/script `setPos`, monster draw-in, event-end positions. WPOS is broadcast to everyone in range, so it is now routed by `UniqueNo` (previously every WPOS moved the local player, e.g. when another player was drawn in); other entities `Warp`. Local placements queue on `LocalPlayerState` and apply at the start of the next locomotion tick, so an in-flight movement tick cannot overwrite them or report a stale position (LSB ignores `0x015` only until the WPOS is sent). Modes per XiPackets: `0x0A` rotates only, `0x08` locks movement until `0x09`/`0x05` (or a zone change), and a charmed player ignores input and takes its own `0x00D` positions.
- **Remote Entity Grounding:** Other players, NPCs and monsters are drawn on the collision floor under their reported position (`EntityGrounding`, same step-up and stair rounding as the local player), not at the reported height: a Windower capture shows a player position-hacked 10 yalms up drawn walking on the floor by another client, before and after a teleport. Actors with the server's GroundFlag (Flags0 bit 15, "ignores world collision", XiPackets) and doors/elevators/ships keep the reported height; display only, server positions untouched.
- **Collision Acceleration Structure:** `ZoneCollisionMesh` indexes the soup in a 4-yalm uniform XZ grid (CSR arrays, allocation-free queries), so a query scans only the triangles in the query cell.
- **Camera Collision:** the orbital camera raycasts from its look-at point to its orbit position against the collision soup (`ZoneCollisionMesh.TryRaycast`, grid cells under the segment, double-sided, skipping camera-transparent triangles) and pulls in 0.3 yalms in front of the first hit; it snaps in and eases back out at 10 yalms/s, so it no longer swings outside cave walls.
